What happens next

We'll read through your details and get back to you, usually the same working day, to arrange a convenient time for a free survey or to talk through your repair.

Helpful things to have ready

  • A photo of the door from outside, and one from inside if you can
  • Rough width and height of the opening (we'll measure properly at the survey)
  • Whether there's a power socket in the garage, if you want an electric door
  • For repairs, a short description of what the door is doing
