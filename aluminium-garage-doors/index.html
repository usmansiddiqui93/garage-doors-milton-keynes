Why choose aluminium garage doors in Milton Keynes?

Aluminium garage doors in Milton Keynes have become one of the most requested options we quote for, and it's easy to see why. Aluminium is light, it doesn't rust the way untreated steel can, and it takes a powder-coated or foiled finish that stays smart for years with nothing more than an occasional wash. It's also the material behind most of the slim, glassy, architectural doors you see on new-build and extended homes around Broughton, Oxley Park and Whitehouse.

Because aluminium is so light, it suits doors that move a lot. A roller door made of aluminium slats puts less load on the motor, and a large glazed sectional door built on an aluminium frame is far easier to balance than a solid timber one of the same size. That lightness is the reason aluminium dominates the roller garage door market in the UK.

Types of aluminium garage door we fit

Aluminium roller garage doors

The most common aluminium door by far. Double-skinned aluminium slats are usually filled with foam insulation, then coil into a compact box above the opening. Nothing swings out onto the drive and nothing hangs from the ceiling, so you keep the full height of the garage for storage, bikes or a roof box. Almost all aluminium roller shutter garage doors are electric, operated by a remote, with a manual override for power cuts.

Aluminium sectional garage doors

Most mainstream sectional garage doors are made from insulated steel panels, but some ranges use aluminium sections or an aluminium frame with infill panels. These are typically chosen for full-vision or part-glazed designs, where glass or polycarbonate sits within slim aluminium profiles. They lift vertically and run back under the ceiling.

Side hinged and sliding aluminium doors

Side hinged aluminium garage doors open like a pair of French doors and are popular where a garage is used as a workshop or home gym. Side-sliding and bifold aluminium doors are available too, usually as made-to-order products for larger or unusual openings — see our made to measure garage doors page for those.

Aluminium up and over doors

These are rare compared with steel or GRP, but a few manufacturers produce them. If you love the up and over style, we'll talk you through whether aluminium or a different material makes more sense for your opening.

Pros and cons of aluminium

  • Corrosion resistance: aluminium forms its own protective oxide layer, so a scratch won't spread into rust. A big plus for exposed driveways.
  • Light weight: easier on motors, springs and bearings, which helps an electric door run smoothly for longer.
  • Modern looks: crisp lines, slim frames and glass make aluminium the go-to for contemporary homes.
  • Low maintenance: no painting or treating — just a wipe with warm, soapy water a few times a year.
  • Dents more easily: aluminium is softer than steel, so a stray football or a car bumper can leave a mark on thinner profiles.
  • Cost: aluminium doors usually cost more than an equivalent steel door, especially glazed designs.
  • Insulation varies: foam-filled roller slats insulate reasonably well, but heavily glazed doors lose more heat than solid insulated panels.

Who are aluminium garage doors best for?

Aluminium suits you if you want an electric roller door that saves space, you're after a contemporary look to match anthracite window frames, or you'd like natural light in the garage. It's a strong choice for homeowners who simply don't want to spend weekends repainting. If you're mostly after strength and the lowest price, a steel door may serve you better; if you want warmth and character, timber might. Our guide to garage door materials compares them side by side, and you can see every style we offer in our full range of garage doors.

Colour matters a lot with aluminium. Powder coating gives you a wide range of RAL shades, from white and cream to black and the very popular anthracite grey. Browse ideas on our modern garage doors and colours page, and if you want a lot of glass, see garage doors with windows.

Can you paint aluminium garage doors?

Yes, but it's rarely necessary. Factory powder coating is tough, and a professional respray is usually better than DIY paint, which can peel from smooth aluminium if the surface isn't properly keyed and primed. If your door has faded badly, we're happy to advise whether a respray or a replacement offers better value. Our article on painting garage doors covers the basics.

Tip: if you're comparing quotes, check whether the price includes the electrical connection, removal of your old door and making good to the brickwork. These are the details that most often separate one quote from another.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Are aluminium garage doors better than steel?

Neither is better in every case. Aluminium is lighter and won't rust, so it suits electric roller doors and glazed designs. Steel is stronger and more resistant to dents, and usually cheaper for a solid up and over or sectional door. We'll explain the trade-offs at your survey so you can choose with confidence.

Are aluminium roller garage doors insulated?

Most are. The slats are typically double-skinned aluminium filled with polyurethane foam, which helps keep the garage warmer and quieter than a single-skin metal door. They don't usually match a thick insulated sectional door, so if heat retention is your priority, see our insulated garage doors page.

How long does it take to fit an aluminium garage door?

Once the door has been made to your measurements, fitting is usually completed in one day for single and double doors. That includes removing your old door, installing the new one, setting up the motor and safety features, and showing you how everything works. Larger glazed or bespoke doors can occasionally need longer.

Do aluminium garage doors dent easily?

They can dent more readily than steel because aluminium is a softer metal, especially thinner roller slats. In everyday use this is rarely a problem, but if children play ball games on the drive or space is tight for parking, we may suggest a heavier-gauge profile or a steel alternative.

Can I get an aluminium garage door in anthracite grey?

Yes. Anthracite grey (RAL 7016) is one of the most popular powder-coated finishes we fit, and it pairs well with modern grey window frames. Black, white, cream and many other RAL colours are available too, as are woodgrain-effect finishes on some ranges. We'll bring a colour chart to your free survey.
