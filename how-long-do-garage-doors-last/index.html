How Long Do Garage Doors Last?

Typical lifespans for each door type and part, the warning signs that a door is near the end, and simple ways to get more years from yours.

Most garage doors last somewhere between 15 and 30 years. A well-made steel, sectional or roller door that is fitted properly and serviced regularly will often reach 20 years or more, while timber doors can last just as long if the finish is kept up. The moving parts wear out sooner: springs, cables, rollers and motors typically need attention or replacement within that time. How long your garage door lasts in practice depends on the quality of the door, how often it is used, the weather it faces and — most of all — how well it is looked after.

How long should garage doors last? Typical lifespans by type

The figures below are realistic ranges based on typical domestic use (a few cycles a day) in UK conditions. They are a guide, not a guarantee; a door on a busy household's main entrance will wear faster than one opened once a week.

Door typeTypical lifespanWhat usually limits it
Steel up and over15–25 yearsRust at the bottom edge, worn springs and pivots
Sectional (insulated steel)20–30 yearsRollers, hinges and springs (all replaceable)
Aluminium roller15–25 yearsMotor wear, damaged laths
Timber (side hinged or up and over)15–30+ yearsRot and movement if the finish is neglected
GRP (fibreglass)20+ yearsFading and surface wear; very rust resistant
Composite / uPVC side door20–30 yearsLocks and hinges

What affects how long a garage door lasts?

  • Build quality: thicker steel, better coatings and heavier-duty hardware simply last longer.
  • Installation: a door that is fitted square, level and correctly balanced wears evenly. A poorly fitted door strains its springs and motor from day one.
  • Usage: a door opened ten times a day reaches its spring and motor limits several times faster than one opened twice.
  • Exposure: doors facing driving rain, coastal air or road spray need more care than sheltered ones.
  • Maintenance: cleaning, lubrication and regular checks are the cheapest way to add years.

How long do the parts last?

Garage doors rarely fail all at once. More often, one component wears out and the rest of the door is perfectly sound, which is why a repair is often better value than a new door.

Springs

Springs are rated by cycles (one open and one close). Standard springs are commonly designed for around 10,000–20,000 cycles. At two or three cycles a day that can mean anything from about 8 to 15 years, but damp, rust and lack of lubrication shorten it. A broken spring is one of the most common garage door faults. Springs and cables are under high tension and can cause serious injury, so they should always be replaced by a professional; our spring and cable repair service covers all major makes.

Cables, rollers and hinges

Lifting cables fray over time, and nylon or steel rollers wear and become noisy. These are usually inexpensive to replace during a service and can add years to a door.

Locks and handles

Locks on manual doors can seize or wear, particularly on older up and over doors. They are normally easy to replace, though parts for very old or discontinued models can be harder to find.

How long do electric garage doors last?

The door itself lasts as long as any manual door of the same type. The motor is the part to think about. A good-quality garage door motor often lasts around 10–15 years in normal domestic use, sometimes longer. Remote control handsets tend to fail earlier through drops and wear, and batteries need changing every year or two.

Things that shorten the life of an automatic garage door include:

  • A door that is out of balance, which forces the motor to work too hard.
  • Dirty or damaged guides and tracks that add friction.
  • Water getting into control boxes or receivers.
  • Power surges, which can damage control boards.
  • Very high usage, for example a garage that doubles as the main entrance to the house.

How long do steel garage doors last?

Modern steel doors are galvanised and finished with a factory coating, so they resist rust well. The weak spot is usually the bottom edge, where water collects and road salt splashes up. With regular washing and touch-ups to any chips, a steel door can easily reach 20 years or more. See our steel garage doors page for current ranges.

How long do timber garage doors last?

Timber is the material where maintenance makes the biggest difference. A hardwood or well-treated softwood door that is refinished every few years can last for decades. One that is left to weather can start to rot at the bottom rail within ten years. Our guide to painting garage doors explains how to keep timber protected.

Hörmann garage doors lifespan and premium brands

Premium manufacturers such as Hörmann build their doors to high specifications, with galvanised steel, quality hardware and well-engineered spring systems, and they often offer long manufacturer warranties on certain models. That does not make them maintenance-free, but it does mean the door panels and frames commonly outlast the moving parts by a long way. Always check the warranty terms that come with a specific door, as these vary by product and may depend on regular servicing.

Signs your garage door is near the end of its life

  • Rust that has gone right through the panel, not just surface marks.
  • Timber that is soft, split or rotten in several places.
  • The door frame or panel is bent, often after a vehicle impact.
  • Parts are no longer available for an old or discontinued model.
  • Repairs are becoming frequent and adding up.
  • Draughts, damp and poor security that a new insulated door would solve.

Not sure which side of the line your door is on? Our guide repair or replace your garage door? sets out the questions to ask.

How to make your garage door last longer

  1. Have it serviced: an annual or two-yearly check catches worn springs, cables and rollers before they fail. Our garage door servicing covers balance, fixings, safety devices and lubrication.
  2. Keep it clean: wash the door a few times a year, especially the bottom edge.
  3. Lubricate the right parts with a suitable product, and keep guides and tracks clean. See our garage door maintenance guide.
  4. Protect the finish: touch up chips on steel and refinish timber before it starts to weather.
  5. Do not ignore small problems: a door that is noisy, slow or uneven is telling you something.
  6. Check the weather seals so water does not sit along the bottom rail.
Tip: if you have just moved into a house, find out the age of the garage door and when it was last serviced. A quick service is a cheap way to establish a baseline and avoid surprises.

When a new door makes more sense

If your door is 20 or more years old, rusting through, or no longer meets your needs for security and insulation, a replacement garage door may be better value than continuing to repair it. Modern doors are warmer, quieter and more secure than most older designs, and an electric option makes everyday use far easier. You can find more help choosing in our garage door guides.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How long do automatic garage doors last?

The door itself typically lasts 15 to 30 years depending on the material and type, just like a manual door. The motor usually lasts around 10 to 15 years in normal home use, and remotes and batteries need replacing more often. Regular servicing that keeps the door balanced and running freely is the best way to help the motor reach the upper end of that range.

How often should garage door springs be replaced?

Springs are replaced when they break or show signs of wear rather than on a fixed schedule. Standard springs are commonly rated for around 10,000 to 20,000 cycles, which for most households means somewhere between 8 and 15 years. Because springs are under high tension and dangerous to handle, replacement should always be carried out by a professional engineer.

Is it worth repairing a 20-year-old garage door?

It can be. If the panel and frame are sound and only a spring, cable, lock or motor has failed, a repair is often good value. If the door is rusting through, rotting, badly bent or needs repeated repairs, a new door is usually the better investment, especially as modern doors are more secure and better insulated. An engineer can give you an honest view.

Do garage doors last longer if they are serviced?

Yes. Servicing keeps the door balanced, replaces worn parts before they cause damage and checks safety features. An unbalanced door puts extra strain on springs, cables and motors, so catching small problems early can add years to the door's life. Many manufacturers also expect regular servicing for warranty purposes, so keep a record of each visit.

How long do wooden garage doors last?

A well-maintained timber garage door can last 15 to 30 years or more. The key is keeping the finish in good condition by recoating it every few years with a suitable exterior paint or stain, and making sure water does not sit along the bottom rail. Neglected timber doors can start to rot within ten years, particularly where they face prevailing weather.
