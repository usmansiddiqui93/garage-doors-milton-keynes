Electric garage door repairs for every type of automatic door

An automatic door is a combination of a mechanical door and an electrical drive, and a fault in either can stop it working. We carry out electric garage door repairs across Milton Keynes on roller doors, sectional doors, automated up and over doors and electric side hinged doors. Our engineers test the power supply, motor, control board, safety devices and the door itself, so we fix the cause rather than just the symptom.

Before you book, it is worth checking a few simple things yourself. Our guide to an electric garage door not working covers safe checks such as fuses, remote batteries and the manual override. If those do not solve it, read on.

Common electric garage door faults we fix

Motor runs but the door does not move

On chain or belt-drive openers fitted to sectional and up and over doors, this often means the drive carriage has been left disengaged after a manual release, or a gear inside the operator has stripped. On a roller door it can point to a failed tubular motor brake or a slipping coupling. We re-engage, repair or replace the drive as needed.

Door starts to close, then reverses

Nearly always a safety system doing its job. Photocells that are dirty, knocked out of line or failing will stop the door closing, as will a damaged safety edge on the bottom of a roller curtain. Force settings that have drifted as the door ages can also cause false reversals. We clean, realign or replace the sensors and re-set the travel limits and force.

Remote control or keypad not working

If fresh batteries do not help, the handset may be faulty or have lost its coding, or the receiver in the motor may have failed. We can recode existing remotes, supply compatible replacement handsets for most major systems and fit external keypads or key switches.

Door completely dead

No lights and no response usually means a supply problem: a tripped spur, a blown fuse in the control unit or a failed transformer. We check the supply and control board and replace components where needed. Any fixed wiring faults beyond the door's own circuit are referred to a qualified electrician.

Noisy, jerky or slow operation

Worn rollers, dry hinges, a slack chain or an unbalanced door all make the motor work harder. Left alone, the extra strain shortens motor life considerably. We adjust and lubricate the mechanics, correct the spring balance and check that the motor is not overheating.

Roller garage door motor repairs

Roller doors are the most common electric doors we see. Their tubular motors sit inside the barrel at the top of the opening, so diagnosis means testing the motor and limit settings rather than simply swapping a box on the ceiling. Typical roller door jobs include:

  • Resetting top and bottom limits after a power cut or manual winding
  • Replacing a burnt-out or seized tubular motor with a matched unit
  • Fitting a new control box, receiver or safety edge
  • Repairing curtains that have come out of the guides, and replacing bent laths

We also deal with roller doors that have been damaged by a car reversing into the curtain. Often only a few laths need replacing rather than the whole curtain, which keeps the cost down. For more on the door type itself see our roller garage doors page.

Openers and operators we work on

We repair openers from the main manufacturers found on UK homes, including Hörmann, Garador, Novoferm, SWS, Gliderol and Somfy-powered systems, along with many generic and older units. Some older operators are no longer supported and spares are scarce. In that case we will explain your options honestly, which may be fitting a modern replacement motor to your existing door rather than replacing the whole door. Our garage door automation service covers new operators in more depth.

How we diagnose an electric door

  1. Check the door first. We release the motor and operate the door by hand. If it is heavy or binding, the problem is mechanical and the motor is a victim, not the cause.
  2. Test power and controls. Supply, fuses, control board, receiver and wall switches are checked in turn.
  3. Test the safety devices. Photocells, safety edges and force sensing are tested so the door reverses correctly on an obstruction.
  4. Explain and price. You get a plain-English explanation and a written price before any work.
Tip: keep a note of the make and model on your motor's label. Sending us a photo when you call means we can often bring the correct part on the first visit.

Repair the motor or replace it?

A motor that has given ten or more years of service has done well, and it is reasonable to ask whether another repair is worth it. As a rule of thumb, we recommend repair when the fault is a single component such as a capacitor, receiver, limit switch or set of photocells, and the operator is still a supported model. We lean towards replacement when the gearbox is worn, the board has failed on an unsupported model, or the motor has already needed several repairs. A modern operator also brings benefits an old one cannot: soft start and stop, rolling-code remotes that are harder to copy, smartphone control on some systems, and optional battery backup for power cuts.

Whatever the answer, we will give you both prices so you can make the call yourself. We never fit a new motor simply because it is easier than fixing the old one.

Electric garage door repair costs

Costs depend on the part and the system. Recoding remotes or realigning photocells is a modest job, while a new tubular motor or operator is a bigger one. You can see typical ranges on our garage door prices guide. If your door has a mechanical fault too, such as a broken spring on a sectional door, we cover that under our general garage door repairs service and can do both on one visit.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Why does my electric garage door go back up when closing?

The safety system is detecting an obstruction. Check that nothing is blocking the photocells and that their lenses are clean and lined up. If that does not help, the sensors, safety edge or force settings may need attention. We can test and adjust them so the door closes reliably while still reversing safely if something is in the way.

Can you replace a lost or broken garage door remote?

Usually, yes. We supply replacement handsets for most major opener systems and code them to your motor on site. If your system is very old and handsets are no longer available, we can fit a new receiver that works with modern remotes, which is often cheaper than replacing the motor.

How much does an electric garage door repair cost?

It depends on the fault. Simple jobs such as recoding remotes or realigning sensors are at the lower end, while a replacement motor or control board costs more. Our garage door prices page shows typical guide ranges, and we always give you a written price after diagnosis and before any work starts.

Can I open my electric garage door during a power cut?

Most doors have a manual override. Sectional and up and over doors normally have a release cord or handle on the motor carriage, and roller doors often have a winding handle or an external release. Our guide to an electric garage door not working explains the options. If yours will not release, call us rather than forcing it.
