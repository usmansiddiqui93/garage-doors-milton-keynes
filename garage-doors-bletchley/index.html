Garage doors Bletchley, from your local Milton Keynes team

If you're looking for garage doors Bletchley homeowners can rely on, our fitters are only a few minutes down the road. We supply, fit, repair and service garage doors across the MK2 and MK3 postcode districts, including Fenny Stratford, Water Eaton, Far Bletchley, West Bletchley, Old Bletchley, Denbigh and the newer streets of Newton Leys, plus the neighbouring parts of MK1. Whether it's a seized up-and-over in a garage block or a new electric door on a detached house, you'll get a clear written quote and a straight answer.

Bletchley packs a lot of different housing into a small area, and that shapes the kind of garage door work we do here.

Bletchley housing and the doors that suit it

Victorian and Edwardian streets

Around the old town centre, Fenny Stratford and the roads near the station there are plenty of older terraces and semis. Garages here tend to be detached or tucked at the end of the garden, often reached from a back lane, and many still have an old timber door or a basic steel canopy door. Openings are rarely a standard size, so made-to-measure garage doors or a traditional pair of side-hinged doors are often the neatest fix.

Post-war and 1970s estates

Bletchley grew quickly after the war as a London overspill town, and later estates such as the Lakes Estate in Water Eaton brought rows of garage blocks with them. Many of those blocks still have their original steel up-and-over doors: rusted along the bottom rail, with tired springs and locks that barely turn. Swapping them for a new up-and-over garage door is one of the most common, and most affordable, jobs we do in the town.

Newer homes at Newton Leys and beyond

The newer developments on the southern edge of Bletchley mostly have integral or attached garages with standard-sized openings. Owners here often want to upgrade a basic builder's door to something warmer, quieter and easier to use, or simply add a motor.

Roller garage doors in Bletchley

Roller doors are a strong choice where the driveway is short or the garage is tight inside, because the curtain coils into a box above the opening instead of swinging out or taking up ceiling space. Our insulated aluminium roller garage doors come in a wide range of colours and are electrically operated on most models, which suits busy households who want to open up from the car.

  • Ideal for short drives and garages used for storage or as a workshop
  • Insulated slats help if the garage is attached to the house
  • Remote controls and a manual override supplied with most systems

Repairs we're called out for most in MK2 and MK3

A lot of Bletchley's doors are 20, 30 or even 40 years old, so repairs make up a big share of our work here. Typical call-outs include:

  • Snapped tension springs or frayed cables on up-and-over doors
  • Doors that drop, stick halfway or won't lock
  • Electric doors with failed remotes, motors or safety sensors
  • Bent panels or frames after a knock from a car

Our garage door repairs service covers all major makes, and we carry common parts on the van. Springs and cables are under serious tension, so please don't try to adjust them yourself: leave the door shut and give us a call. If a door isn't worth saving, we'll say so honestly and price a replacement alongside the repair so you can compare.

Automating an existing door

If your door is in decent condition but you're tired of heaving it up by hand, we can often fit a motor to it rather than replace it. Automating an existing garage door works well on many up-and-over and sectional doors, and we'll check the door is properly balanced and safe before we quote.

Getting to you

Bletchley is right on our doorstep, so survey visits are easy to arrange and we can often get to repairs the same or next day. Just over the way, we also cover garage doors in Woburn Sands to the east and Leighton Buzzard to the south, and you can see every town on our areas we cover page.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Which parts of Bletchley do you cover?

All of it. We regularly work across MK1, MK2 and MK3, including Fenny Stratford, Water Eaton, Far Bletchley, West Bletchley, Old Bletchley, Denbigh and Newton Leys. We also cover the villages just outside town, such as Bow Brickhill and Drayton Parslow. Because Bletchley is so close to our Milton Keynes base, surveys are quick to book and repairs are often possible the same or next day.

Can you replace a door in a garage block?

Yes, it's one of our most common Bletchley jobs. We measure the opening, check the condition of the frame and brickwork, and fit a new up-and-over, roller or sectional door to suit. If the garage is rented from a council or housing association, check you have their permission before changing the door. We're happy to quote for a single garage or a whole row.

How much does a new garage door cost in Bletchley?

As a rough guide, a standard single steel up-and-over door is typically around £700 to £1,300 supplied and fitted, while an insulated electric roller door is usually £1,600 to £2,800. Size, colour and extras all affect the price, so these are indicative ranges only. We confirm an exact figure after a free survey. See our garage door prices page for more detail.

Is a roller door suitable for an older Bletchley garage?

Often, yes. Roller doors need a little space above the opening for the coil box, but they can usually be fitted behind or inside the opening, which suits older garages with awkward sizes or uneven brickwork. On some very low garages a different door type works better. We'll check headroom, side room and the condition of the walls during the survey before recommending anything.

Will you repair my old door rather than sell me a new one?

If it's worth repairing, absolutely. Plenty of older doors only need new springs, cables, rollers or a lock to give years more use. Where a door is badly rusted, unsafe or would cost nearly as much to fix as to replace, we'll tell you plainly and give you both options in writing so you can decide without any pressure.
