Why garage door servicing in Milton Keynes matters

Garage doors are among the largest moving objects in a house, and most get used more than any other door. Yet many go years without anyone looking at them until something snaps. Our garage door servicing in Milton Keynes is a thorough inspection and adjustment that keeps the door working as it should, helps the springs and motor last longer and confirms that the safety features still protect your family.

We service manual and electric doors of every major type and make, from 1980s canopy doors to modern insulated sectional and roller doors.

What a garage door service includes

Every service follows the same checklist, adapted to your door type. You get a short written note of what we found and anything that needs attention.

Door and frame

  • Check the frame is square, secure and properly fixed to the masonry
  • Inspect panels, laths or leaves for damage, rust, rot or splits
  • Tighten fixings, hinges, brackets and pivot points
  • Check bottom seals, brush seals and weather strips

Springs, cables and balance

  • Inspect torsion or tension springs for gaps, rust and fatigue
  • Check cables for fraying and drums or pulleys for wear
  • Test the door's balance and adjust spring tension where safe and necessary

Tracks, rollers and moving parts

  • Clean and check tracks and guide rails for alignment and damage
  • Inspect rollers, bearings and hinges, and replace worn items if you approve
  • Lubricate moving parts with the correct product for the door type

Locks and security

  • Test the lock, handle and latch bars or shoot bolts
  • Adjust keeps and latches so the door secures fully

Electric operators

  • Test the motor, drive, chain or belt tension and control board
  • Check travel limits and obstruction force settings
  • Test photocells, safety edges and auto-reverse
  • Check remotes, keypads and the manual release
  • Test the battery backup unit, where fitted

The benefits of regular servicing

  • Safety. Auto-reverse, photocells and spring balance are checked so the door cannot close on a person, pet or car.
  • Fewer breakdowns. Frayed cables, cracked rollers and loose fixings are spotted before they fail.
  • Longer motor life. A balanced, freely running door puts far less strain on an electric operator.
  • Quieter running. Correct lubrication and adjustment stop the squeals and rattles that tend to creep in over time.
  • Better security and weather sealing. Locks that close properly and seals that meet the floor keep out intruders, rain and draughts.

How often should a garage door be serviced?

For most homes, once a year is right. That suits a typical household opening the door a few times a day. We suggest more frequent visits in a few situations:

  • Heavy use – if the garage is your main entrance, or the door cycles many times a day, every six months is sensible.
  • Electric doors with older motors – motors more than about ten years old benefit from closer attention to force settings and wear.
  • Exposed or coastal conditions – less of an issue in Milton Keynes, but doors facing prevailing weather or on dusty sites need more cleaning and lubrication.

If your door is only used occasionally, a service every 12 to 18 months is usually fine, though springs and cables still age with time.

Roller garage door maintenance

Roller doors need a slightly different approach. The curtain should not be greased like an up and over door's pivots; instead the guide rails are cleaned and treated with a dry, silicone-based lubricant so dust does not stick. We check the end caps, brush seals and safety edge, test the tubular motor and limits, and confirm the manual override works. Our roller garage doors page has more about how they are built.

What lubricant is best for garage doors?

We get asked this a lot. For hinges, rollers and torsion springs we use a light lithium or garage-door-specific spray that does not attract grit. For roller door guides and nylon parts we use a dry silicone spray. We avoid heavy grease on tracks, because it collects dirt and makes things worse. Our guide on how to maintain garage doors explains the simple jobs you can do between visits.

Servicing electric garage doors

Electric doors add a layer of electronics and safety equipment that needs periodic testing. Over time, as springs relax and rollers wear, the door gets heavier and the motor compensates by working harder. The obstruction force can drift upwards without anyone noticing, which means the door would push harder against an obstacle before reversing. During a service we measure how the door behaves, rebalance it, and re-set the force and limits so the operator is gentle and reliable. We also test the backup battery, if your system has one, since these lose capacity with age and you only find out during a power cut. For faults found during the visit, our electric garage door repairs team can usually help straight away.

Servicing all major makes

We service doors from Henderson, Cardale, Garador, Hörmann, Novoferm, SWS, Gliderol and other manufacturers, as well as unbranded doors. See our garage door brands section for more on each make. Following the manufacturer's maintenance advice can also help if you ever need to rely on a product warranty, so keep a note of each service.

Servicing for landlords and new homeowners

If you let a property, an annual service gives you a dated record that the garage door was inspected, which is useful evidence that you have taken reasonable care of a heavy moving item your tenants use every day. If you have just bought a home, a first service is a sensible way to find out what make of door you have, how old the springs and motor are and whether anything needs budgeting for. Many buyers on newer Milton Keynes developments find their door has never been touched since the builder fitted it.

When a service turns up a problem

Sometimes we find a frayed cable, cracked roller or failing sensor. We will show you, explain the risk and give you a price. You decide whether to have it done there and then or later. Anything that makes the door unsafe will be pointed out clearly. If the door is near the end of its life, our guide on how long garage doors last will help you plan ahead, and our repairs team can fix most faults on the spot.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How long does a garage door service take?

Most services take around an hour for a single manual door. Electric doors and double doors take a little longer because there are more parts and safety features to test. If we find a fault that needs a part, we can often fit it on the same visit once you have agreed the price.

Do you service electric roller garage doors?

Yes. Electric roller garage door maintenance includes cleaning the guide rails, checking the curtain and end caps, testing the tubular motor and limit settings, and confirming the safety edge, photocells and manual override work correctly. We use dry lubricants on roller guides so dust and grit do not build up.

Is a service worth it on an older door?

Often more so. Older doors have aged springs, cables and rollers, and a service spots wear before something breaks. It also gives you an honest picture of the door's condition, so you can budget for a replacement if it is near the end of its life instead of being caught out.

Can I service my garage door myself?

You can safely do basic upkeep: cleaning tracks, keeping seals clear, lubricating hinges and testing the auto-reverse with a soft object. Anything involving springs, cables or tension adjustment should be left to a professional because of the stored energy involved. Our maintenance guide covers the safe tasks in detail.
