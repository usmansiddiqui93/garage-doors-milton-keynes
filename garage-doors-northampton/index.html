Garage doors Northampton: where we work

We supply, fit, repair and service garage doors Northampton homeowners can rely on, right across the NN1 to NN7 postcode districts. That covers the town centre and older areas such as Abington, St James, Semilong and Far Cotton; established suburbs like Kingsthorpe, Duston and Weston Favell; the eastern district estates; the southern districts including East Hunsbury, West Hunsbury and Grange Park; newer homes at Upton; and villages such as Moulton, Roade, Blisworth and Hackleton. The M1 brings us up from Milton Keynes quickly.

Northampton's housing and its garages

Victorian terraces and villas

Northampton's shoe-making heritage left it with large areas of Victorian terraces and villas. Garages here are usually at the rear, reached from back lanes or service roads, with narrow or uneven openings.

The eastern and southern districts

From the 1970s onwards Northampton expanded with large planned estates, first to the east, in areas such as Lings, Thorplands, Ecton Brook and Rectory Farm, and later to the south around Hunsbury. Many homes here have attached garages or share garage courts, and a lot still have tired original steel up-and-over doors.

Newer homes

Developments like Upton and Grange Park have mostly integral and attached garages built to standard sizes, where owners want warm, quiet and automated doors.

Roller and roller shutter doors

For terraced streets with rear lanes and homes with short drives, roller garage doors are hard to beat. The insulated aluminium curtain coils into a box above the opening, so nothing swings out, and most come with a motor and remote. People often call them roller shutter doors, but domestic versions are far quieter and better insulated than industrial shutters.

Sectional garage doors

Insulated sectional garage doors are one of the most popular upgrades for Northampton's newer homes. They rise vertically and slide back under the ceiling, seal on all four sides and come in many finishes. We supply sectional doors from leading manufacturers including Hörmann and Novoferm; read about the Hörmann garage door range for an idea of what's possible.

Side-hinged and GRP doors

Side-hinged (or side-hung) doors open like a pair of large French doors and suit older garages and anyone who wants quick access without opening the whole door. Our side-hinged garage doors come in timber, steel and GRP. GRP garage doors are particularly practical: they look like timber, but won't rot, warp or rust.

Up-and-over, steel and electric doors

A new steel up-and-over door from makers such as Garador or Cardale remains the best-value way to replace an old door on the estates. If you'd like to stop lifting by hand, we fit electric garage doors in roller, sectional and up-and-over styles, or can add a motor to a suitable existing door.

Double garages in the villages and new estates

Larger homes in villages like Moulton, Roade and Blisworth, and many of the detached houses at Grange Park and Upton, have double garages. A single wide sectional or roller door gives plenty of room for two cars, while two single doors keep a central pier and let you open only one side. We can advise on which suits your drive, your vehicles and your budget.

Repairs and servicing

We repair all major makes, from broken springs and snapped cables to failed motors, damaged panels and jammed locks, and carry common parts on the van. A regular garage door service helps catch worn components before they fail. Tensioned springs and cables can cause serious injury, so please leave them to a professional.

Northampton is the largest town in our area, and we cover it end to end: established suburbs such as Kingsthorpe, Duston, Weston Favell, Hunsbury and Wootton, newer developments like Upton and Grange Park, and villages on the edge of town such as Moulton. Garages here range from 1960s and 70s garage blocks to integral garages on modern estates.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Which parts of Northampton do you cover?

We cover NN1 to NN7, including the town centre, Abington, St James, Semilong, Far Cotton, Kingsthorpe, Duston, Weston Favell, the eastern district estates, East and West Hunsbury, Grange Park and Upton, plus villages such as Moulton, Roade, Blisworth and Hackleton. If you're further out, send us your postcode and we'll confirm whether we can help.

How much does a new garage door cost in Northampton?

As an indicative guide, a single steel up-and-over door typically costs around £700 to £1,300 supplied and fitted, an insulated electric roller door around £1,600 to £2,800, and an insulated sectional door around £1,800 to £3,500. Your price depends on size, design and extras. We confirm a fixed written quote after a free survey. See our garage door prices page for more.

What's the difference between side-hinged and side-hung doors?

There's no real difference: both terms describe garage doors that open outwards on hinges at each side, like a pair of large French doors. Some open evenly in the middle, others split into one wide and one narrow leaf. They're great for older garages, workshops and anyone who wants to pop in without opening the whole door.

Can you replace doors in a garage court on an estate?

Yes. Many Northampton estates have courts of garages with old steel doors, and replacing them is a common job for us. We'll check the frames and brickwork and fit new doors to suit. If the garages belong to a council, housing association or management company, check you have permission first. We can quote neighbours together if several of you want new doors.

Do you repair garage doors you didn't fit in Northampton?

Yes. We repair all common makes and types, whoever fitted them, including up and over, roller, sectional and side hinged doors and their motors. If a repair isn't worth it, we'll tell you honestly and quote for a replacement instead.
