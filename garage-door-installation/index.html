Garage doors supplied and fitted, start to finish

Buying a door online and finding someone to fit it can leave you stuck in the middle when sizes are wrong or parts are missing. When you have garage doors supplied and fitted by us, one team is responsible for everything: measuring, ordering, delivery, installation and aftercare. We install doors on existing homes and new openings across Milton Keynes and nearby towns, including Northampton and the wider Northamptonshire area.

Doors we supply and install

  • Up and over garage doors in steel, GRP and timber, in canopy and retractable styles
  • Sectional doors, insulated and electric, in ribbed, smooth and panelled designs
  • Roller doors with insulated aluminium laths and tubular motors
  • Side hinged garage doors in steel, GRP, timber and composite
  • Wooden garage doors in a range of timbers and finishes
  • uPVC and composite doors, including matching pedestrian side doors

We source doors from leading UK and European manufacturers, which means we can match the door to your budget and style rather than pushing a single brand.

Why measuring properly matters

Most installation problems start with a bad measurement. A garage door is sized to the structural opening, but the right door also depends on the headroom above it, the side room either side, the depth of the garage and whether the opening is square. We check all of these at the survey. If your opening is a non-standard size, which is common on older homes and some self-builds, we can order a made-to-measure door instead of forcing a standard size to fit. Our guide to garage door sizes in the UK explains the common dimensions.

Fitting positions

Doors can be fitted in three main positions:

  • Behind the opening – the frame sits on the inside face of the wall, so the door can be slightly larger than the opening. Common for sectional and roller doors.
  • Between the piers – the frame fits within the opening, flush with the brickwork. Common for up and over doors.
  • In front of the opening – occasionally used for roller doors where internal space is very limited.

We recommend the position that gives you the most usable opening width and height for your car.

What happens on installation day

  1. Arrival and protection. We protect the drive and anything stored near the door, and agree where the old door will go while we work.
  2. Removing the existing door. If you are replacing a door, we safely release its springs and remove it with its frame. Our garage door replacement page explains that side of the job.
  3. Preparing the opening. We check the brickwork and lintel, and pack or trim the opening so the new frame sits square and plumb.
  4. Fitting the frame and door. Frames are fixed securely into the masonry, then tracks, springs, the door leaf or curtain and the locks are fitted.
  5. Electrics and setup. For electric doors we connect the motor to a suitable fused supply provided by your electrician or arranged beforehand, set the travel limits and force, and code your remotes.
  6. Sealing and finishing. Weather seals and trims are fitted and gaps finished neatly so the door looks right from the street.
  7. Handover. We demonstrate the door, locks, remotes and manual release, and leave you with the manufacturer's information.

Electric doors and power supplies

Electric garage doors need a fused spur within reach of the motor. Many garages already have a socket that can be used or adapted, but if there is no power in the garage, a qualified electrician will need to provide one before fitting day. We will tell you exactly what is required at the survey so there are no surprises. If you would rather keep your current door and just add a motor, see our garage door automation service.

New openings, extensions and new builds

We also install doors where there has never been one before, such as a new garage on an extension or a self-build. In these cases timing matters. We can survey the opening once the brickwork and lintel are in, advise the builder on finished opening sizes and any timber sub-frame, and then fit once the floor screed and render are complete. If you are at the planning stage, talk to us early about headroom and side room so the opening suits the door you want, particularly for sectional doors with ceiling-mounted tracks.

Matching side doors

Many garages have a pedestrian door at the side or back as well. We can supply and fit a matching steel, composite or timber side door in the same colour and style as your new garage door, so the whole building looks finished. Fitting both on the same visit saves time and keeps the look consistent.

Supply only or supply and fit?

We focus on supply and fit because that is how we can stand behind the whole job. When we measure, order and install the door, any problem is ours to fix, and the door's guarantee is not put at risk by incorrect installation. Many manufacturers also expect their doors to be installed by competent fitters for warranty purposes. Guarantee details are provided with your quote.

Supply and fit prices

Garage door prices fitted vary with the door type, size, material, insulation and operation. For example, a manual steel up and over door costs much less than an electric insulated sectional in a bespoke colour. You will find typical ranges for each type on our garage door prices guide. Your fitted price is always confirmed in writing after the free survey, with fitting, removal and disposal included where applicable.

Aftercare once your door is fitted

A new door needs very little attention, but it is not maintenance-free. We recommend keeping tracks and seals clear of leaves and grit, washing painted steel a couple of times a year and having the door serviced annually so the springs, locks and any motor stay correctly adjusted. If anything does not feel right in the first weeks, such as a seal not meeting the floor or a remote that needs recoding, call us and we will come back and put it right.

What a good installation includes

A new door is only as good as the way it is fitted. On every installation we check the opening is square, fix the frame securely into sound brickwork, set the springs or counterbalance correctly, and seal the gaps at the sides and bottom against draughts and rain. Electric doors are set up with the safety features working (the door should stop and reverse if it meets an obstruction) and we show you how to use the manual release in case of a power cut. Finally we tidy up and take the old door away.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How long does garage door installation take?

Most single doors are fitted in a day. Double doors, electric doors and openings that need the brickwork prepared or a lintel checked can take a little longer. We confirm the expected time on your quote so you can plan around it, and we always clear up fully before we leave.

Do I need an electrician for an electric garage door?

Only if there is no suitable power supply in the garage. The motor needs a fused spur nearby. If one is already there, we connect to it. If not, a qualified electrician should install one before fitting day. We explain exactly what is needed at the survey so the installation runs smoothly.

Can you fit a garage door to a non-standard opening?

Yes. Many older homes and self-builds have openings that do not match standard sizes. We can order made-to-measure doors from several manufacturers, or choose a fitting position, such as behind the opening, that suits the space. Accurate measuring at the survey is the key, and we take care of that for you.

Do you install garage doors in Northamptonshire?

Yes. We supply and fit garage doors in Northampton and Towcester and across the surrounding Northamptonshire villages, as well as throughout Milton Keynes, north Buckinghamshire and parts of Bedfordshire. Get in touch with your postcode and we will confirm we cover your area.
