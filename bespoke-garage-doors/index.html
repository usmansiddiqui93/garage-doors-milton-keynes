Bespoke garage doors in Milton Keynes, made to fit properly

Bespoke garage doors in Milton Keynes are the answer when an off-the-shelf door simply won't do. Perhaps your opening is an odd size, the brickwork isn't square, the garage sits in a conservation area, or you want a design that nobody else on the street has. A made to measure garage door is manufactured to the exact dimensions we take on site, so it fits snugly, seals properly and looks as though it was always meant to be there.

"Bespoke" covers a wide spectrum. At one end it simply means a standard design built to a non-standard size, which many manufacturers offer at a modest extra cost. At the other end it means a fully custom door — hand-made timber, a unique panel layout, special glazing or a side sliding mechanism — designed around your home.

When do you need a made to measure garage door?

  • Non-standard openings: older garages, especially those built before metric sizes became common, often fall between standard widths and heights.
  • Low headroom or tight side room: some door types need space above or beside the opening, so the door has to be specified carefully.
  • Period and listed properties: traditional coach-house or barn-style doors in the right timber can be essential for planning or simply to suit the building.
  • Arched or shaped openings: curved heads need a door built to follow the shape.
  • Design ambitions: flush cladding to match the house, a hidden door, or large glazed panels.

Our UK garage door sizes guide lists common standard dimensions, which is a handy first check before you assume you need bespoke.

Bespoke options we can supply and fit

Bespoke wooden and timber garage doors

The most popular custom request. Made to measure wooden garage doors can be built in hardwoods such as oak or iroko, softwoods such as larch or cedar, or engineered timber that resists movement. You choose the style — vertical boarding, framed and braced, panelled, or with glazed top lights — plus the finish, ironmongery and locks. A bespoke oak garage door on a stone cottage around Olney or Stony Stratford is hard to beat. Read more on our timber garage doors page.

Made to measure side hinged garage doors

Ideal for openings with no headroom, or where you walk in and out all day. Leaves can be split equally or unequally, and a pedestrian leaf can be built in. See side hinged garage doors for how these work.

Made to measure sectional and roller doors

Most sectional and roller garage doors are already manufactured to order in a wide range of sizes, so a precise fit doesn't always mean a big premium. Bespoke timber-faced sectional doors are also available if you want real wood with an up-and-in action.

Bespoke side sliding garage doors

A side sliding door runs on a track and curves around the inside wall of the garage. It needs no headroom and lets you open just a section to walk in. These are usually made to order and suit long, narrow garages.

Made to measure up and over doors

Steel and GRP up and over doors can be made in non-standard sizes, often with a timber or steel sub-frame to fill an awkward gap neatly.

Advantages and downsides of going bespoke

The benefits are a precise fit with minimal packing or filler, better weather sealing, a design that suits your property, and the freedom to choose materials, colours and hardware exactly as you want them.

The trade-offs are longer lead times — a fully custom timber door can take several weeks to make — and a higher price than a stock door. Custom doors are also built for your opening and can't be returned once made, which is why accurate measuring matters so much.

Who chooses custom garage doors?

Bespoke doors are popular with owners of period homes and barn conversions, people renovating or extending who want the garage to match new windows and cladding, and anyone with an opening that's been botched in the past and never had a door that fitted properly. They're also a smart choice if you're planning a home office or gym in the garage and want insulation, glazing and a pedestrian door all in one design. For contemporary ideas — flush panels, slim glass, anthracite or black finishes — look at our modern garage doors page.

Our measuring, design and fitting process

  1. Free survey: we take precise measurements of the opening, check it for square and level, note headroom, side room and floor condition, and photograph the elevation.
  2. Design discussion: we talk through style, material, colour, glazing, locks and automation, and explain what will and won't work for your opening.
  3. Written quote and drawing: you get a clear specification before anything is ordered.
  4. Manufacture: lead times depend on the material and maker; we'll give you a realistic estimate.
  5. Installation: we remove the old door, prepare the opening, fit the new door and adjust it carefully. Timber doors may need their final coats of finish on site, and we'll explain the aftercare.
Planning note: if your home is listed or in a conservation area, check with the local planning authority before changing the appearance of the garage. A like-for-like replacement is often straightforward, but it's always worth asking first.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How much more does a made to measure garage door cost?

For many steel, GRP, roller and sectional doors, a made to measure size adds only a modest premium because they're already built to order. Fully custom timber, shaped or side sliding doors cost more, often several thousand pounds depending on materials and size. We give you a clear written price after measuring, so there are no surprises.

How long does a bespoke garage door take?

Doors that are simply made to a non-standard size are often ready in a few weeks. Fully bespoke timber or side sliding doors usually take longer because they're designed and hand-built for your opening. We'll give you a realistic lead time when we quote and keep you updated on progress, then fitting is usually completed in a day or two.

Can you make a garage door for an arched opening?

Yes. Arched and shaped openings are a common reason for going bespoke. Timber side hinged doors are usually the best fit, as the head of each leaf can be shaped to follow the curve. Some up and over doors can be fitted behind a shaped opening too. We'll template the arch carefully during the survey.

Do bespoke timber garage doors need much maintenance?

Timber needs more care than steel or composite. Expect to clean the door and refresh the stain or paint every few years, more often on south-facing or exposed doors. Engineered timber and good factory finishes reduce movement and extend the time between coats. Our painting garage doors guide explains what's involved.

Can a bespoke garage door be electric?

Often, yes. Timber-faced sectional doors, made to measure roller doors and many up and over doors can all be automated. Side hinged and side sliding doors can also be motorised with the right operator, although it depends on weight and layout. We'll tell you at the design stage whether automation is practical for your door.
