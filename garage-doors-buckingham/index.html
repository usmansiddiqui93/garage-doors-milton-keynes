Buckingham's mix of homes

Buckingham is a compact market town wrapped around a loop of the River Great Ouse, home to the University of Buckingham and the Old Gaol. Its housing ranges widely:

  • Georgian and Victorian properties in and around the town centre, often with detached garages or converted outbuildings.
  • Post-war and 1970s to 1990s estates, many with attached garages or shared garage blocks fitted with steel up-and-over doors.
  • Newer developments such as Lace Hill on the southern side of town, mostly with integral or attached garages.
  • Village homes and barn-style properties where larger or double garages are common.

Replacement garage doors in Buckingham

Most of our Buckingham work is replacing doors that have reached the end of the road. Our garage door replacement service covers the lot: survey, ordering the right door, removing and disposing of the old one, and fitting the new door and frame. A standard single door is usually fitted in a day.

When you're choosing a replacement, it's worth thinking about how you actually use the garage. If it's for the car, convenience and a short drive might point you to a sectional or roller door. If it's mostly storage, a good-quality up-and-over may be all you need.

Up and over garage doors in Buckingham

The up and over garage door is still the most common style on Buckingham's estates, and for good reason: it's simple, strong and the best-value way to replace an old door. Modern versions come in a wide choice of panel designs and colours, with better locking than the doors they replace. Most are made from galvanised, pre-finished steel, which keeps the cost down and the maintenance low.

Garage door installation done properly

A good door can be let down by poor fitting. Our garage door installation work includes checking the opening is square, fixing securely into sound brickwork or a proper timber sub-frame, setting the springs or torsion correctly, and finishing neatly with seals and trims. We test everything and show you how the door and any remotes work before we leave.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How much is a replacement garage door in Buckingham?

As an indicative guide, a single steel up-and-over replacement typically costs around £700 to £1,300 supplied and fitted, while insulated roller and sectional doors usually start from around £1,600 to £1,800. Size, design, colour and automation all affect the final figure. We give you a fixed written quote after a free survey, so there are no surprises on the day.

Do you offer a mobile repair service around Buckingham?

Yes. All our repairs are carried out at your home. Our engineers carry common springs, cables, rollers, locks and remote handsets, so many faults are fixed on the first visit. If a specific part needs ordering we'll make the door safe, explain what's needed and return to finish the job as soon as the part arrives.

Which villages around Buckingham do you cover?

We regularly work across the MK18 villages, including Maids Moreton, Gawcott, Tingewick, Padbury, Akeley and Thornborough, as well as the newer Lace Hill development and the rest of Buckingham town. If you're a little further out, get in touch anyway, because we can usually still help.

What's the best garage door for a Buckingham new-build?

It depends on how you use the garage. If you park in it daily, an insulated sectional or roller door with a motor is the most convenient and keeps an integral garage warmer. If it's mainly storage, a quality up-and-over is often perfectly adequate and more affordable. We'll ask about your drive, ceiling space and budget before suggesting a door.

Will you take away my old garage door?

Yes. When we replace a door, removing and disposing of the old door and frame is included in our quote, so you don't need to arrange a skip or a trip to the tip. We'll also tidy up afterwards, leaving the garage and drive clear. If you'd prefer to keep any parts of the old door, just let the fitter know.
