About SWS and SeceuroGlide

SWS is a British manufacturer of garage doors, and SeceuroGlide is its best-known range of roller garage doors. SeceuroGlide doors are built from aluminium slats filled with insulating foam, which coil around a barrel inside a box above the opening. They are designed to be electrically operated, with remote control as standard on most installations.

SWS has built a reputation around security and insulation in a roller door, and its range also extends to sectional doors.

The SWS SeceuroGlide range

SeceuroGlide roller garage doors

The core of the range is the SeceuroGlide insulated roller door, available in a wide choice of colours and in widths that suit single and double garages. Because the door rolls up vertically, it needs no space in front of the garage and very little inside, making it a strong choice for shorter driveways or garages used for storage. Learn more on our roller garage doors page.

Higher-security SeceuroGlide models

SWS offers higher-security versions, such as SeceuroGlide Excel, which have been tested to recognised security standards. LPS 1175, for example, is a Loss Prevention Certification Board standard that rates how well a product resists attack for a set time with specific tools. We will confirm exactly which certification any model carries at quote stage. See our secure garage doors page and our guide are roller garage doors secure? for more.

SWS sectional doors

For those who prefer the look of a panelled door, SWS also offers sectional garage doors, which lift vertically and run back under the garage ceiling.

Common SeceuroGlide faults

  • Door stops or reverses: often a dirty or misaligned photocell, a triggered safety edge, or limits that need resetting.
  • Remote not working: flat batteries, a handset that has lost its pairing, or a fault with the receiver.
  • Door will not move during a power cut: most doors have a manual override, and we can show you how to use yours safely.
  • Noisy running or slats out of line: usually worn guides, debris in the channels or a damaged slat.

For a step-by-step checklist before you call, see electric garage door not working. If the door still will not run, book our electric garage door repairs service. We aim to attend quickly, often the same or next day.

Is a SeceuroGlide door right for your garage?

A SeceuroGlide roller door suits most single and double garages, but we check a few things at survey: enough headroom for the coil box, a sound lintel and piers to fix into, and a power supply for the motor. Doors can usually be fitted behind the opening or, where space is tight, in front of it.

SWS compared with other brands

SeceuroGlide is a roller door specialist, while brands such as Hörmann and Novoferm offer roller doors alongside wide sectional and up and over ranges. See all makes on our brands page.

Repairs and servicing for SWS roller doors

Roller doors are reliable, but the motor, remotes, safety edge and photocells all need to be working for the door to run properly. Common issues we see are remotes that have lost their programming, safety edges that stop the door closing, and laths damaged by a car bump. We diagnose the fault, explain the cost before doing the work, and can usually source parts for SWS doors. See our electric garage door repairs page for more.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Who makes SeceuroGlide garage doors?

SeceuroGlide roller garage doors are made by SWS, a British garage door manufacturer. The doors use foam-filled aluminium slats that roll into a compact box above the opening and are usually electrically operated. We supply, fit, repair and service SeceuroGlide doors as an independent installer, not as an official SWS dealer.

Are SWS SeceuroGlide roller doors secure?

Standard SeceuroGlide doors are robust, and SWS also offers higher-security models tested to recognised standards such as LPS 1175. The level of protection depends on the model, so we will confirm the certification of any door we quote. Fitting quality matters too, which is why we fix guides and the box securely into sound masonry.

Can you replace a lost SeceuroGlide remote?

Yes, in most cases. We can supply a compatible handset and programme it to your door's receiver on site. If a remote has been lost or stolen, we can usually clear the old codes so that handset no longer works. Older control units may need checking first, and we will explain any options before ordering.

How do I open my SeceuroGlide door in a power cut?

Most SeceuroGlide doors have a manual override, typically a winding handle or similar mechanism, so the door can be raised and lowered without power. The exact method depends on how your door was fitted. If you are unsure, or the override is stiff, contact us and we will show you safely rather than forcing anything.

How much does a SeceuroGlide roller door cost?

As a guide, a single electric SeceuroGlide roller door typically costs around £1,600 to £2,800 fitted, with higher-security models and double widths costing more. Colour, size and the condition of the opening affect the final price. We confirm your price in writing after a free, no-obligation survey.
