My garage door is broken or not working properly

Start with garage door repairs. We repair up and over, roller, sectional and side hinged doors from all major manufacturers, including locks, handles, rollers, tracks and panels. We aim to get to repairs quickly, often the same or next day.

  • If the door suddenly became very heavy, dropped, or you heard a loud bang, it is likely a spring or cable problem. Do not keep using the door — springs and cables are under high tension and should be dealt with by a professional.
  • If it is an electric door that won't open, close or respond to the remote, see electric garage door repairs. Our guide to an electric garage door not working also covers a few safe checks you can do first.

My door works but could be better

Garage door servicing is a planned check and adjustment: we inspect springs, cables, rollers, hinges and fixings, lubricate moving parts, check balance and safety features, and tell you about anything that is wearing. Regular servicing can head off breakdowns and extend the life of the door.

If you are happy with your door but tired of lifting it by hand, garage door automation adds an electric operator and remote controls to many existing doors.

I want a new garage door

If your door is past its best, our garage door replacement service takes care of everything, from measuring to removing and disposing of the old door. Our supply and fit installation page explains what happens on fitting day. If you are unsure whether to fix or replace, read our guide on repairing versus replacing a garage door.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Do you repair all makes of garage door?

Yes. We repair all major makes, including doors from Hörmann, Garador, Cardale, Henderson, Novoferm and SWS, along with many older and less common doors. Some discontinued doors need parts that are hard to find; if that is the case we will explain the options, which may include a compatible replacement part or, if it makes more sense, a new door.

How quickly can you come out for a garage door repair?

We aim to get to repairs quickly — often the same or next day, depending on where you are and how busy we are. If your door is stuck open and your home is insecure, tell us when you call so we can prioritise. Sending a photo of the door beforehand helps us bring the right parts.

How often should a garage door be serviced?

Most manufacturers recommend an annual service for a garage door, especially electric doors and doors with springs and cables. A service checks balance, fixings and safety features, lubricates moving parts and catches worn components early. Doors that are used many times a day may benefit from more frequent checks.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace a garage door?

A repair is usually cheaper in the short term, and many problems such as a broken cable, worn rollers or a faulty remote are inexpensive to fix. Replacement becomes better value when the door is badly corroded, rotten, damaged or repeatedly failing, or when you want insulation or electric operation. We will give you an honest view after inspecting the door.
