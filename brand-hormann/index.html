About Hörmann

Hörmann is a family-owned German manufacturer and one of the best-known names in garage doors across Europe. Alongside garage doors, the group makes entrance doors, industrial doors and the operators (motors) that drive them. In the UK, Hörmann doors are a common sight on everything from modest semis to high-specification new homes, and the group also owns the British brand Garador.

What people tend to like about Hörmann is consistency: well-engineered running gear, a broad choice of finishes and sizes, and doors and openers designed to work together.

The Hörmann garage door range

Hörmann sectional garage doors

Sectional doors are probably what Hörmann is best known for. The door is made of insulated panels that lift vertically and run back under the garage ceiling, so you can park right up to the door and nothing swings out onto the drive. There is a wide choice of panel styles (ribbed, plain, cassette and others), surface finishes and colours, plus glazing options. If you want to compare this style generally, see our page on sectional garage doors.

Hörmann up and over garage doors

Hörmann also makes steel up and over doors, a practical choice for replacing an older canopy or retractable door like-for-like. They come in a range of designs and colours and can be automated. Our up and over garage doors page explains canopy versus retractable gear.

Hörmann roller garage doors

Hörmann roller doors coil into a compact box above the opening, which suits garages with limited headroom or where you want to keep ceiling space free for storage. They are normally electrically operated.

Side hinged doors and openers

The range also includes side hinged doors, plus Hörmann's own garage door operators and remote controls. Hörmann operators can also be fitted to many existing manual doors, which we cover below.

Common Hörmann repairs we see

Most Hörmann call-outs we attend fall into a few familiar groups:

  • A sectional door that suddenly feels very heavy or will only lift part way, which usually means a broken spring.
  • A door that closes unevenly or sits crooked, often a cable issue.
  • Remotes that have stopped working, need reprogramming or have been lost.
  • An operator that reverses before it fully closes, often due to the safety settings, a dirty photocell or a track obstruction.

We aim to get to repairs quickly, often the same or next day, and we explain what is wrong and what it will cost before we start. If you are not sure whether a door is worth fixing, our guide to repairing or replacing a garage door helps you weigh it up.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Are you an official Hörmann dealer?

No. We are an independent local garage door company. We can supply and fit Hörmann doors and operators, repair and service existing Hörmann doors and source spare parts, but we do not claim official dealer or approved installer status. We explain exactly how the manufacturer's warranty and our own workmanship guarantee apply in your written quote.

Can you repair an older Hörmann garage door?

In most cases, yes. Springs, cables, rollers, seals, handles and locks on Hörmann doors can usually be replaced, and we can often source parts for older models. If a door is badly corroded or parts are no longer available, we will tell you honestly and give you a price for a replacement as well, so you can choose.

Can you replace a lost Hörmann remote control?

Usually, yes. We can supply a compatible Hörmann hand transmitter and programme it to your operator, and we can also clear old remotes from the system if one has been lost or stolen. If your operator is very old, we will check compatibility first and explain your options, including upgrading the motor.

Do Hörmann sectional garage doors need servicing?

Like any door with springs and moving parts, a Hörmann sectional door benefits from a regular service, typically once a year. We check spring balance, cables, rollers and fixings, lubricate the moving parts and test the operator's safety reversal. See our garage door maintenance guide for simple checks you can do between visits.

How long does it take to fit a new Hörmann door?

Most single Hörmann doors are fitted in a day, including removing your old door and tidying up. Larger double sectional doors or jobs that need extra preparation to the opening can take longer. Lead times depend on the model and finish you choose, and we will give you a realistic estimate when we confirm your order.
