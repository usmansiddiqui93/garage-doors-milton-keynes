Garage Doors Across Buckinghamshire, Bedfordshire & Northamptonshire

We supply, fit, repair and service garage doors in Buckinghamshire and the neighbouring parts of Bedfordshire and Northamptonshire, working outwards from Milton Keynes. Because Milton Keynes sits close to the borders of all three counties, our fitters can reach a wide area. Here is an overview of where we work.

Milton Keynes and north Buckinghamshire

Milton Keynes is our home patch. We cover all the city's estates and grid squares, and the older towns that became part of it, including Bletchley, Wolverton, Stony Stratford and Newport Pagnell, as well as Olney and Woburn Sands. Further into Buckinghamshire we cover the market towns of Buckingham and Winslow, and south to Aylesbury.

Garage doors in Bedfordshire

East of Milton Keynes, we work in Cranfield and the villages around it, in Leighton Buzzard to the south, and in Bedford. Bedfordshire has a real mix of housing, from Victorian terraces with rear garages to large modern developments with integral garages, so we fit everything from compact roller doors to wide insulated sectional doors.

Garage doors in Northamptonshire

To the north, we cover Towcester and the surrounding villages, and Northampton. We supply and fit roller, sectional, up and over and side hinged doors across this area, along with electric operators, and repair all major makes, including Garador, Hörmann, Cardale and Novoferm doors.

Typical garage door jobs across the region

The housing stock varies a lot across our patch, and so do the jobs we see. In the older market towns, single detached garages and garages in rear courts often still have original steel or timber up and over doors, and the most common calls are for snapped cables, worn springs and locks that have seized. On newer estates, integral garages are more usual, and homeowners tend to ask for insulated roller or sectional doors in anthracite grey or white to match modern windows.

Rural villages bring their own challenges: timber doors on period properties, uneven openings that need a made-to-measure door, and longer drives where an electric door with a remote saves getting out of the car in bad weather. Whatever the setting, we measure every opening at a free survey before recommending anything.

Travel and response times

Because we work outwards from Milton Keynes, the city and its nearest towns are the quickest for us to reach. For repairs, we aim to get to you quickly, often the same or next day, and we will give you a realistic time when you call. For new doors, surveys are booked at a time that suits you, and fitting dates depend on the manufacturer's lead time rather than where you live.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Do you cover the whole of Milton Keynes?

Yes. We cover all of Milton Keynes, including the central grid-square estates, newer developments on the edges of the city, and older towns such as Bletchley, Wolverton, Stony Stratford and Newport Pagnell. Give us your postcode when you enquire and we will arrange a convenient time for a free survey or repair visit.

Do you work outside Buckinghamshire?

Yes. As well as Buckinghamshire towns such as Buckingham, Winslow and Aylesbury, we cover parts of Bedfordshire, including Bedford, Cranfield and Leighton Buzzard, and Northamptonshire, including Northampton and Towcester. If your town is not listed, get in touch and we will let you know whether we can help.

Do you charge more for areas further from Milton Keynes?

For new doors, surveys are free across the areas we cover and the price in your written quote is the price you pay. For repairs further from Milton Keynes, we will tell you about any call-out charge up front, before we visit, so there are no surprises.

Can you supply the same door brands in every area?

Yes. The doors we supply and fit are the same wherever you live in our coverage area, including models from leading manufacturers such as Hörmann, Garador, Novoferm and SWS. Lead times depend on the manufacturer and specification rather than your location.

Do you charge more for areas outside Milton Keynes?

Surveys are free across the areas we cover. For repairs, we explain any call-out charge up front before booking, so there are no surprises whichever town you're in.
