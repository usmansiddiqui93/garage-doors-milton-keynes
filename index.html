Garage doors we supply and fit in Milton Keynes

Every garage is different, so the right door depends on your opening, how much headroom and driveway space you have, how you use the garage and your budget. Here are the main options, with links to more detail on each.

Roller garage doors

Roller garage doors (sometimes called roller shutter garage doors) coil up into a compact box above the opening, so they take up no ceiling space and nothing swings out onto the drive. Most are made from insulated aluminium slats and are usually electrically operated. They are a popular choice on Milton Keynes estates where driveways are short.

Sectional garage doors

Sectional garage doors are made of horizontal insulated panels that lift vertically and then run back along the ceiling. They seal well, are quiet to operate and come in a wide range of styles and colours. They suit modern homes and wider openings, and work very well with electric operation.

Up and over garage doors

Up and over garage doors are the classic single-panel door found on many homes in the area. They are the most budget-friendly way to replace a tired door and are available in steel, GRP and timber, in canopy and retractable gear types.

Side hinged garage doors

Side hinged garage doors open outwards like a pair of large doors. They are handy if you use the garage as a workshop or need regular pedestrian access, and they look right on traditional and period properties.

Materials, styles and special options

Alongside the door type, you can choose the material and finish. We fit steel garage doors for value and strength, wooden and timber garage doors for a natural look, low-maintenance GRP garage doors, and insulated garage doors if the garage is attached to the house or used as a gym or workspace. If security is your priority, we also fit secure garage doors tested to recognised standards, and wide doors for double garages.

Garage door repairs, servicing and automation

A new door is not always the answer. Plenty of doors can be put right with a repair or a service, and we will tell you honestly if that is the better option.

  • Garage door repairs — stuck, noisy, dropping or out-of-line doors. We repair all major makes and aim to get to repairs quickly, often the same or next day.
  • Spring and cable repairs — broken springs and snapped cables are common on older up and over and sectional doors. These parts are under high tension and should only be handled by a trained engineer.
  • Electric garage door repairs — motor faults, remotes that have stopped working, safety sensor problems and doors that reverse or won't close.
  • Garage door servicing — a check, adjustment and lubrication that helps your door run smoothly and last longer.
  • Garage door automation — many existing up and over, sectional and side hinged doors can be fitted with an electric operator and remote controls.

If you are weighing up whether to fix an old door or start again, our guide on whether to repair or replace a garage door explains what we look for.

Electric and automatic garage doors in Milton Keynes

Remote control garage doors are now the most requested option we quote for. Roller and sectional doors are usually supplied with a motor as standard, and we can automate many other door types too. Benefits include easy access in bad weather, no heavy lifting, and better security because the door locks down when it closes. Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How much do new garage doors cost in Milton Keynes?

As a rough guide, a manual steel up and over door typically costs £750–£1,400 fitted, while insulated electric roller or sectional doors usually range from about £1,600 to £3,800. Size, material, colour and extras all affect the price. These are indicative figures; we confirm your exact price in a written quote after a free survey. See our garage door prices page for more detail.

Do you repair garage doors you didn't fit?

Yes. We repair all major makes of garage door, whether we fitted them or not. That includes up and over, roller, sectional and side hinged doors, along with springs, cables, locks, motors and remote controls. We aim to get to repairs quickly, often the same or next day, and we will always tell you if a repair is not worth doing.

Can you make my existing garage door electric?

In many cases, yes. Most up and over, sectional and side hinged doors in good condition can be automated with an electric operator and remote controls. We check the door is balanced and running smoothly first, because a motor will not cure a door that is already struggling. Read more on our garage door automation page.

How long does it take to fit a new garage door?

Most single garage door replacements are fitted in a day, including removing and taking away the old door. Larger or more complex jobs, such as double doors, bespoke timber doors or work that needs the opening altered, can take longer. Lead times for the door itself vary by manufacturer and specification, and we will give you an estimate with your quote.
