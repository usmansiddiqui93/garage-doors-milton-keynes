There are a handful of garage door brands that most UK homeowners will come across, and each has its own strengths. We supply and fit doors from leading manufacturers and repair all major makes, so we can give you a straightforward view of the options without pushing one brand over another. This page explains how to think about brands, and you will find a page on each one below.

Does the brand matter?

The brand matters less than getting the right type of door, correctly sized and properly fitted. A good-quality door installed badly will give trouble, while a sensibly chosen mid-range door fitted well will last for many years. That said, brands do differ in their range, styles, finishes, insulation values, security options and how easy it is to get parts later on.

A quick overview of the main brands

  • Hörmann — a large German manufacturer with an extensive range, best known in the UK for sectional doors as well as up and over, roller and side hinged doors and its own electric operators.
  • Garador — a long-established UK name, particularly well known for steel up and over doors, with side hinged, sectional, roller and electric options too.
  • Cardale — a familiar name on many older UK garage doors, especially up and over doors. We repair and service Cardale doors and can advise on replacements.
  • Novoferm — a European manufacturer offering sectional, up and over and roller doors along with operators.
  • SWS / SeceuroGlide — a UK manufacturer known for insulated aluminium roller garage doors, including SeceuroGlide models with security-tested options.
  • Henderson — a name found on many older up and over doors in the UK. We regularly repair Henderson doors, including springs, cables and locks.

Choosing a brand for a new door

When we quote for a new door, we usually start with the type you want — for example sectional garage doors or roller garage doors — then suggest models from different manufacturers that suit your opening, style and budget. If security is important, ask about doors tested to recognised standards; our secure garage doors page explains what those standards mean.

Repairs to older and discontinued doors

Many garages around Milton Keynes still have original doors from brands that have changed hands or ranges that are no longer made. We can often source compatible parts for these. When parts are no longer available, we will explain the options honestly. Our guide on whether to repair or replace a garage door may help.

Not sure what make your door is? Look for a label on the frame, the inside of the door panel or the motor unit, and send us a photo with your enquiry. Get in touch here.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

What is the best garage door brand in the UK?

There is no single best brand for everyone. Hörmann is known for its broad sectional range, Garador for up and over doors, and SWS for insulated roller doors, for example. The best door for you depends on the type, size, style, insulation and security you need and your budget. We can compare suitable models at your free survey.

Can you repair a garage door if the brand no longer exists?

Often, yes. Many older doors use standard components such as springs, cables, rollers and locks that can be replaced with compatible parts. If a specific part is no longer made and there is no suitable alternative, we will explain this clearly and talk you through options, including replacement if that offers better value.

Are you an official dealer for these brands?

We are an independent local business. We supply and fit doors from leading manufacturers and repair all major makes, but we do not claim official dealer or approved installer status with any brand. Manufacturer guarantee details for any door we supply will be set out with your quote.

How do I find out what brand my garage door is?

Check for a sticker or plate on the inside of the door panel, on the frame, or on the motor unit if it is electric. Some brands also stamp their name on handles or locks. If you cannot find anything, send us a few photos of the door, inside and out, and we can usually identify it.
