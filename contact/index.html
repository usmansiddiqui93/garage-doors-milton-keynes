Whether you need a new door, a repair or a service, the quickest way to get a garage door quote in Milton Keynes is to call 01908 000000 or fill in the form below. The more you tell us, the more useful our first reply will be.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Your postcode — so we can confirm we cover your area. See areas we cover.
  • What you need — a new door, a repair, a service or automation of your existing door.
  • Your current door type — up and over, roller, sectional or side hinged, and the make if you know it.
  • Rough opening size — width and height in millimetres if you can. Our guide to garage door sizes explains how.
  • Photos — one of the outside, one of the inside showing the mechanism, and a close-up of any damage or label.
  • What you would like — for example an insulated electric door, a particular colour, or extra security.

What happens next

  1. We get back to you to talk through your enquiry and answer any questions.
  2. For new doors, we book a free survey at a time that suits you to measure up and discuss options.
  3. You receive a clear written quote, including guarantee details. There is no obligation to go ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Quotes and surveys for new garage doors are free and come with no obligation. After the survey you receive a clear written quote showing the door, fitting, removal of your old door and guarantee details. For repairs, we will explain any call-out charge before we visit, so you know where you stand.

Can you give me a price without a survey?

We can often give a rough guide price from photos and measurements, which helps you decide whether to go further. For new doors, we always confirm the final price after a free survey, because the exact size, headroom, fixings and condition of the opening all affect what door is suitable and what fitting involves.

How long does a survey take?

A typical survey for a single garage door takes around 30 to 45 minutes. We measure the opening, check headroom and side room, look at the condition of the frame and brickwork, and talk through door types, colours and options with you. Double doors or more complex openings may take a little longer.

Do I need to be at home for the survey?

It is best if someone can be there, so we can get into the garage, check the inside measurements and power supply, and talk through options with you. If that is difficult, let us know and we will try to arrange a time that works for you.
