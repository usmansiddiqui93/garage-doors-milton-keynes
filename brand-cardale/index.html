We repair and service Cardale garage doors in Milton Keynes and the surrounding area, source spare parts where they are still available, automate suitable doors and fit modern replacements when a door is beyond economical repair.

About Cardale

Cardale is a long-established British garage door brand, best known for steel up and over doors. Cardale doors were fitted to a great many UK homes and garage blocks, so plenty of them are still in daily use, including on the estates around Milton Keynes, Bedford and Northampton.

Because many Cardale doors we see are older, the questions we get are usually practical: can it be fixed, can I still get parts, and is it worth it? That is where we focus.

Cardale doors we commonly work on

Cardale up and over garage doors

Most Cardale doors are steel up and over designs on canopy or retractable gear, in single and double widths. They use springs and, depending on the mechanism, cables to counterbalance the weight of the door. When those parts wear, the door becomes heavy, lifts unevenly or will not stay open. Our up and over garage doors page explains how the two gear types differ.

Other Cardale styles

We also come across other Cardale doors, such as roller and side hinged models, on some properties. Whatever the style, we will inspect it and tell you what is wrong, what parts it needs and whether repair makes sense.

Repair or replace your Cardale door?

A Cardale door with a sound frame and steel skin is often well worth repairing. New springs and cables, plus a proper adjustment, can make an old door feel light again. It is usually time to think about replacing when:

  • The steel is rusted through along the bottom edge or around the frame.
  • The door panel is badly dented or twisted and no longer sits square.
  • Key parts cannot be sourced, even as compatible alternatives.
  • You want better security, insulation or an electric door.

Our guide on whether to repair or replace a garage door goes into more detail. If you do replace, you might also compare the Garador range or doors from Hörmann, both of which offer steel up and over doors.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Can you still get parts for Cardale garage doors?

For many Cardale doors, yes. Springs, cables, locks and handles are often available either as original parts or as compatible alternatives that fit the existing mechanism. Some very old or unusual models are harder to supply. We will check what your door needs before quoting, and if parts are not available we will explain your replacement options.

Why has my Cardale garage door become hard to lift?

A door that suddenly feels heavy usually has a broken or weakened spring, and sometimes a damaged cable as well. These parts hold a lot of stored energy and can cause serious injury if handled incorrectly, so stop using the door and do not attempt to adjust it. We can inspect it and replace the parts safely.

Is it worth repairing an old Cardale door?

Often it is. If the steel panel and frame are in decent condition, replacing springs, cables or a lock is far cheaper than a new door and can keep it going for years. If the door is rusted through or badly distorted, a replacement is usually better value. We give honest advice and can quote for both options.

Can you replace my Cardale door with something similar?

Yes. We supply and fit steel up and over doors from leading manufacturers in a range of panel designs and colours, so you can choose something close to your existing door or go for a different style altogether. Many people also take the chance to upgrade to an insulated or electric garage door.

Do you service Cardale doors as well as repair them?

Yes. A service involves checking the springs, cables, pivots and fixings, lubricating moving parts, adjusting the balance and testing the lock. It is a good way to catch worn parts before they fail. Older doors in particular benefit from an annual check, and we will flag anything that is likely to need attention soon.
