Garage doors Aylesbury and the surrounding villages

We supply, fit and repair garage doors Aylesbury homeowners can rely on, across the HP19, HP20, HP21 and HP22 postcode districts. That includes established areas such as Walton, Southcourt, Bedgrove and Quarrendon, newer neighbourhoods like Fairford Leys, Watermead, Berryfields and Kingsbrook, and villages including Bierton, Aston Clinton, Weston Turville, Stoke Mandeville and Wendover. Aylesbury is the county town of Buckinghamshire, and we travel down from Milton Keynes regularly for surveys, installations and repairs.

Aylesbury homes and their garages

Aylesbury has grown a great deal since the 1960s, so its garages vary widely:

  • Older homes near the centre and in the villages, often with detached garages or outbuildings and non-standard openings.
  • Post-war and 1960s to 1980s estates, with attached garages, garage blocks and plenty of ageing steel up-and-over doors.
  • Newer developments such as Fairford Leys, Berryfields and Kingsbrook, mostly with integral or attached garages to standard sizes.

Sectional garage doors in Aylesbury

Insulated sectional garage doors are among the most popular choices for Aylesbury's newer homes. They lift straight up and run back under the ceiling, so you can park right up to them, and they seal tightly on all four sides. Most are fitted with a motor and remote. Where ceiling space is limited, a side-sliding sectional door, which runs sideways along the garage wall, can be a clever alternative. We supply sectional doors from leading makers including Hörmann.

Roller doors and SWS SeceuroGlide

For short drives or garages full of storage, insulated aluminium roller garage doors are a great fit. The curtain coils into a compact box above the opening, leaving the ceiling free. We supply roller doors from several manufacturers, including the SWS SeceuroGlide range, which includes models tested to recognised security standards.

Side-hinged and wooden doors

For older homes and village properties, a pair of side-hinged garage doors looks traditional and needs no overhead tracks. They're available in timber, steel and GRP. Real wooden doors can be made to measure and suit cottages and period houses in villages like Wendover and Weston Turville, while GRP gives a woodgrain look with far less maintenance.

Secure and insulated doors

If your garage holds bikes, tools or a car, security matters. Modern secure garage doors offer multi-point locking, anti-lift protection and, on electric doors, motors that hold the door firmly closed. Some are independently tested to standards such as LPS 1175, which assesses resistance to forced entry.

Meanwhile, an insulated garage door can make an integral garage noticeably warmer and drier, especially useful if it's a utility room or home gym.

Automated doors and automating existing ones

You can choose a new electric door or add a motor to the door you have. Our garage door automation service fits operators to suitable up-and-over and sectional doors, once we've checked they're balanced and in good condition.

Upgrading a new-build door

Many of the homes in Aylesbury's newer neighbourhoods were handed over with a basic, manual steel door. It's perfectly serviceable, but it's often draughty and noisy, and it can let down an otherwise smart frontage. Replacing it with an insulated, motorised sectional or roller door in a colour that matches your windows is one of the most noticeable improvements you can make to the front of a house, and it can usually be done in a day.

Up-and-over doors and repairs

A new steel up-and-over remains the most affordable replacement for older estate garages. We also repair all major makes, including broken springs and cables, failed locks and electric doors that won't respond. Springs and cables are under high tension, so please leave them to us.

Aylesbury neighbourhoods and new developments

Aylesbury has grown quickly, and we cover both the established parts of town, such as Bedgrove, Southcourt and Walton, and the large newer developments including Berryfields, Kingsbrook, Fairford Leys and Watermead. We also cover nearby villages and towns such as Wendover, Stoke Mandeville and Bierton.

  • Established homes: roller doors where driveway space is tight, and side hinged doors for everyday access.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Which parts of Aylesbury do you cover?

We cover HP19 to HP22, including Walton, Southcourt, Bedgrove, Quarrendon, Fairford Leys, Watermead, Berryfields and Kingsbrook, plus villages such as Bierton, Aston Clinton, Weston Turville, Stoke Mandeville and Wendover. If you're slightly outside these areas, send us your postcode and we'll let you know whether we can help.

What is a side-sliding sectional garage door?

It's a sectional door that opens sideways rather than upwards. The panels run on a track and slide around the corner along one garage wall, leaving the ceiling completely clear. It's useful where there's limited headroom, a loft hatch or storage overhead, or where you want to open just part of the door for pedestrian access. It needs enough clear wall space on one side.

Are SWS SeceuroGlide roller doors secure?

SeceuroGlide roller doors from SWS are designed with security in mind, and some models in the range are tested to recognised standards such as LPS 1175 and Secured by Design requirements. Like any door, the level of protection depends on the model and specification chosen. We'll explain the options and help you choose one that suits your home and budget.

How much does a new garage door cost in Aylesbury?

As an indicative guide, a single steel up-and-over door typically costs around £700 to £1,300 supplied and fitted, an insulated electric roller around £1,600 to £2,800 and an insulated sectional door around £1,800 to £3,500. Your price depends on size, design and extras. We confirm a fixed written quote after a free survey.

What's the most popular garage door in Aylesbury?

On the newer estates, insulated electric sectional doors in anthracite grey or white are the most common choice. In older parts of town, electric roller doors are popular because they don't swing out onto the drive. We'll talk through the options at your free survey.
