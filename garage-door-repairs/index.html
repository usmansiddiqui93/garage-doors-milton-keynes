Garage door repairs Milton Keynes homeowners can rely on

When a garage door fails it usually happens at the worst moment: the car is trapped inside, the door is hanging crooked or it simply will not lock. We carry out garage door repairs in Milton Keynes and the surrounding towns on all the common door types, whatever the make or age. Most faults can be fixed on the first visit because our vans carry the springs, cables, rollers, locks and motor parts that fail most often.

Below we have set out the faults we see most by door type and what we actually do to put them right, so you know what to expect before you pick up the phone.

Common faults by door type and how we fix them

Up and over garage door repairs

Canopy and retractable up and over doors make up a large share of the garages on Milton Keynes estates, and many are 20 or 30 years old. Typical problems include:

  • Door drops or slams shut – usually a broken tension spring or a stretched cable. We replace springs in matched pairs so the door stays balanced.
  • Door opens on one side only – a snapped or slipped cable, or a cone that has come loose on a torsion-spring door. We refit or renew the cables and rebalance the door.
  • Grinding or sticking halfway – worn nylon rollers, bent tracks or a twisted frame. We realign or replace the track and fit new rollers.
  • Handle turns but nothing happens – a failed lock, broken latch bars or perished rods. We fit a new lock mechanism and adjust the latches so the door secures properly.

Springs and cables carry a lot of stored energy, so we cover them in more depth on our spring and cable replacement page.

Roller garage door repairs

Roller doors are mostly electric, so faults tend to be a mix of mechanical and electrical. We regularly deal with slats that have jumped out of the guide rails after a knock from a car, end caps worn through, curtains that wind on unevenly, and motors that hum but will not lift. We can re-seat or replace damaged laths, fit new guide rails and brush seals, and test or replace the tubular motor where needed.

Sectional door repairs

Sectional doors rely on torsion springs above the opening, lifting cables, drums and a run of hinges and rollers. When a torsion spring breaks you will often hear a loud bang and the door becomes very heavy. We replace broken springs, renew frayed cables, swap worn hinges and rollers, and re-set the door's balance before checking the operator's force settings.

Side hinged and wooden garage door repairs

Traditional side hinged doors suffer from sagging leaves, dropped hinges, swollen timber and locks that no longer line up with their keeps. For timber doors we can rehang leaves, replace rotten bottom rails or weatherboards where the rest of the door is sound, and refit shoot bolts and locks. If the timber is too far gone we will tell you honestly and price a replacement as an alternative.

Electric and automatic door faults

Remotes that stop working, photocells that will not align, doors that reverse for no reason and motors that run but do not move the door are all everyday call-outs. Our dedicated electric garage door repairs page explains the diagnosis process in detail.

Doors that will not close or lock

A garage that cannot be secured is a real worry, particularly if it has an internal door into the house or holds bikes, tools and garden equipment. These jobs go to the front of our queue. If we cannot complete a full repair straight away, for example because a specific panel or motor has to be ordered, we will make the door safe and lockable first so you are not left with an open garage overnight. We will also tell you whether the problem is likely to affect your home insurance, and our guide on whether home insurance covers garage doors gives more background.

What happens when you call us

  1. Tell us what is happening. A quick description and, if you can, a photo of the door and any label on the motor or frame helps us bring the right parts.
  2. We book a visit. We aim to get to repairs quickly, often the same or next day, and sooner for doors that will not close or lock.
  3. Diagnosis and a clear price. Our engineer inspects the whole door, explains the fault and gives you a written price before any work starts.
  4. Repair and test. We fix the fault, lubricate the moving parts, check the balance and safety features and run the door through several cycles with you.

We repair all major makes

We work on doors from the manufacturers you are most likely to have in Milton Keynes, including Garador, Henderson, Cardale, Hörmann, Novoferm, SWS and Gliderol, along with older and unbranded doors. Henderson and Cardale parts are a frequent request because so many of their up and over doors are still in service. Where an original part is no longer made we can usually source a compatible replacement.

Is it worth repairing, or time to replace?

Most doors are worth repairing. A new cable, pair of springs or lock costs a fraction of a new door. Replacement starts to make sense when the panel is rusted through, the frame is distorted, the timber is rotten or you are paying for repeat call-outs. Our guide on whether garage doors can be repaired or should be replaced walks through the decision, and if a new door is the better option we can price a replacement garage door on the same visit.

Garage door repair prices

Every repair is priced individually after we have seen the door, but you can see typical costs for cables, springs, locks, motors and remotes on our garage door prices page. There are no hidden extras: the price we agree is the price you pay, and guarantee details are provided with your quote.

Safety first: if a spring or cable has broken, do not try to lift the door by hand or force it closed. Leave it where it is, keep children and pets clear, and call us.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How quickly can you repair my garage door?

We aim to get to repairs quickly, often the same or next day. Doors that will not close or lock, or that have trapped a car inside, are prioritised. Availability depends on the day's bookings and where you are, so call 01908 000000 and we will give you an honest arrival window.

Do you repair all makes of garage door?

Yes. We repair all major makes, including Garador, Henderson, Cardale, Hörmann, Novoferm, SWS and Gliderol, as well as older and unbranded doors. If a part for an older door is no longer produced, we can usually find a compatible alternative that keeps the door working safely.

Can you repair an up and over garage door that has dropped on one side?

Usually, yes. A door that opens lopsided normally has a snapped or slipped cable, or a loose cone on a torsion-spring mechanism. We replace the cables, check the springs and rebalance the door. Please do not keep operating it, because a lopsided door can twist its frame and turn a small repair into a bigger one.

Will you tell me the price before you start?

Always. Our engineer diagnoses the fault first, explains what is wrong and gives you a clear written price. Nothing is done until you agree. If a repair would not be good value compared with a new door, we will say so and can quote for both options so you can decide.

Do you repair wooden garage doors?

Yes. We rehang sagging timber doors, replace failed hinges and locks, and can renew rotten bottom rails or weatherboards when the rest of the door is sound. Where rot has spread through the frame, we will advise whether repair is sensible or whether a new timber or low-maintenance door would serve you better.
