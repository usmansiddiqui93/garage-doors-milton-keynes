About Garador

Garador is a long-established British garage door manufacturer and part of the Hörmann group, the German manufacturer we cover on our Hörmann garage doors page. Garador is especially well known for steel up and over doors, which have been fitted to huge numbers of UK homes, but the range today goes well beyond that.

The Garador garage door range

Garador up and over garage doors

Garador's steel up and over doors are the classic British garage door: a single panel that tilts up and over on either canopy or retractable gear. There are many panel designs, from simple horizontal ribs to more traditional styles, in a choice of colours. They remain a cost-effective way to replace an old door like-for-like, and they can be automated. Read more about this style on our up and over garage doors page.

Garador side hinged garage doors

Side hinged doors open outwards like a pair of large doors, leaving the full width and height of the opening clear with no gear inside the garage. Garador offers side hinged doors in steel and other materials, which suit garages used as workshops or for storage. See side hinged garage doors for how they compare.

Garador sectional and roller doors

For homeowners who want an insulated door that lifts straight up and keeps the drive clear, Garador also offers sectional doors, and its roller doors coil neatly above the opening. Both are usually electrically operated.

Other materials and openers

As well as steel, Garador has offered doors in timber and GRP, along with its own electric operators and remote controls. Range details change over time, so we will confirm the current models, colours and options when we survey.

Repairing an older Garador door

Many Garador up and over doors we see are 20 or 30 years old and still basically sound. Common problems include a door that has become heavy because a spring has weakened or snapped, a lock that has seized, or a door that catches on one side. Often a new pair of springs, a fresh set of cables and a proper adjustment bring it back to life for far less than a new door. Where the steel skin is rusted through or the frame is distorted, we will say so and quote for a replacement too. Our guide to how long garage doors last covers the signs to look for.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Is Garador part of Hörmann?

Yes. Garador is a British garage door brand within the Hörmann group. The two ranges are separate, with their own designs and models, but they share the same parent company. We can supply, fit and repair doors from both, and we are happy to talk you through how they compare for your garage.

Can you get spare parts for an old Garador up and over door?

Often, yes. Springs, cables, lock mechanisms and handles for many Garador doors are still available as genuine or compatible parts. Very old or discontinued models can be harder, but we will check before we quote. If parts cannot be sourced, we will explain the cost of a replacement door so you can decide.

Can my Garador up and over door be made electric?

Many can. Retractable up and over doors in good condition are usually straightforward to automate, and some canopy doors can be too, depending on the gear and how well the door is balanced. We check the door first, because an operator should never be used to overcome a badly balanced door. See our automation service for more.

My Garador door has become very heavy. What is wrong?

The most common cause is a weakened or broken spring, sometimes with a damaged cable. Springs are under significant tension and can cause serious injury, so please do not try to adjust or remove them yourself. Stop using the door if it is difficult to lift, and call us to inspect and replace the springs safely.

How much does a new Garador garage door cost?

As a guide, a single steel Garador up and over door typically costs around £800 to £1,500 fitted, with electric sectional and roller doors from around £1,800. Size, design, colour, insulation and automation all affect the final figure. We confirm your exact price in writing after a free, no-obligation survey.
