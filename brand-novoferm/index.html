About Novoferm

Novoferm is a major European door manufacturer with German roots, and it is part of the Japanese Sanwa group. As well as residential garage doors, Novoferm makes industrial doors and door operators. In the UK its doors are found on a wide range of homes, and its range covers all the main garage door styles.

The Novoferm garage door range

Novoferm sectional garage doors

Novoferm's sectional doors are made from insulated panels that rise vertically and run back under the ceiling. They keep the driveway clear, seal well around the edges and come in a choice of panel designs and colours. Read our general guide to sectional garage doors to see if the style suits your garage.

Novoferm up and over doors

Steel up and over doors remain popular for straightforward replacements. Novoferm offers them in a variety of designs, and many can be fitted with an operator for remote control.

Novoferm roller and side hinged doors

Novoferm also offers roller doors, which coil compactly above the opening and suit garages with restricted headroom, and side hinged doors, which open like traditional doors and leave the ceiling clear. Compare the roller style on our roller garage doors page.

Operators and remotes

Novoferm makes its own garage door operators and handsets. We can fit these to new doors, and in many cases to suitable existing manual doors.

Repairs to existing Novoferm doors

Typical Novoferm repairs include sectional doors that have lost their balance after a spring breaks, cables that have come off their drums, a door that runs noisily or catches in the tracks, and handsets that need replacing or reprogramming. We aim to get to repair calls quickly, often the same or next day, and always explain the fault and price before starting. If your electric door has stopped, try the checks in our guide to an electric garage door not working first.

Comparing Novoferm with other brands

Many people we survey are weighing Novoferm against other European makes such as Hörmann, or looking at a UK-made roller door from SWS SeceuroGlide. Each has its strengths, and we are happy to explain the differences in plain terms. Browse all makes on our brands page.

Choosing the right Novoferm door

When we survey, we look at more than just the width and height of the opening. Headroom above the opening, side room either side, the depth of the garage and whether you park inside all affect which Novoferm door will suit. We also ask how you use the garage: a workshop or gym benefits more from an insulated sectional door, while a garage used mainly for storage may be well served by a simple steel up and over. If you want a feel for the options before we visit, our guide to types of garage doors sets out the pros and cons of each style.

Servicing a Novoferm door

A yearly service keeps a Novoferm door running smoothly and safely. We check and adjust the springs or counterbalance, inspect cables, rollers and hinges, lubricate moving parts, and test the motor's safety reverse on electric doors. Small problems found early are much cheaper to fix than a broken spring or a door that has come off its track. See our garage door servicing page for what's included.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Who makes Novoferm garage doors?

Novoferm is a European door manufacturer with German roots and is part of the Japanese Sanwa group. It produces residential garage doors, industrial doors and operators. We are an independent installer, not an official Novoferm dealer, but we can supply and fit Novoferm doors and repair and service existing ones across Milton Keynes.

Can you repair a Novoferm sectional door with a broken spring?

Yes. Sectional doors use torsion springs that hold a lot of stored energy, so a broken one should only be replaced by a trained engineer with the right tools. Stop using the door, and particularly do not let an electric operator try to lift it. We will replace the springs, check the cables and rebalance the door.

Can I get a replacement Novoferm remote?

In most cases, yes. We can source a compatible handset for your Novoferm operator and programme it on site. If your operator is an older model, we will check compatibility first. If a remote has been lost or stolen, we can usually clear old handsets from the operator's memory as well.

Is a Novoferm sectional or roller door better for my garage?

Both lift vertically and keep the drive clear. A sectional door usually offers more panel designs and runs under the ceiling, while a roller door coils into a box above the opening, keeping the ceiling free and suiting low headroom. We will look at your garage's headroom, side room and how you use it before recommending either.

How much is a Novoferm garage door?

As a rough guide, a single Novoferm steel up and over door typically costs around £850 to £1,600 fitted, while an electric single sectional is often around £1,900 to £3,500. Size, finish, insulation and automation all change the price. We confirm the exact figure in writing after a free, no-obligation survey.
