Garage doors Olney homeowners can trust

When it comes to garage doors Olney is a town where the look really matters. With its stone-built High Street, wide Market Place and plenty of period cottages, a cheap-looking door can stick out a mile. We supply, fit, repair and service garage doors across the MK46 postcode district, covering Olney itself and nearby villages such as Emberton, Weston Underwood, Lavendon, Clifton Reynes, Warrington and Ravenstone. Every job starts with a free survey and a clear written quote.

Choosing a door for older Olney properties

Many of Olney's older homes are built of local limestone, and their garages are often converted outbuildings, former coach houses or detached garages set back behind the house. These spaces usually have non-standard openings and benefit from a door with traditional proportions.

Timber garage doors

Nothing sits better against old stone than real wood. Our wooden garage doors come in a range of timbers and styles, from simple vertical boarding to framed and panelled designs, and can be made to measure for awkward openings. Timber does need occasional re-finishing, but a well-made door will age gracefully.

Side-hinged doors

A pair of side-hinged garage doors is the classic look for a period garage and suits openings where there's no room for tracks or springs overhead. They're handy for everyday access too, because you can open just one leaf to fetch the bike or the lawnmower.

GRP doors for the timber look without the upkeep

If you love the look of wood but don't want to repaint every few years, GRP garage doors are moulded with a realistic woodgrain finish. They won't rot, warp or rust, which makes them a practical choice for garages close to the river or in shaded spots.

Worth checking: parts of Olney are within a conservation area and some homes are listed. If your property is listed or you're planning a big change to how the front of the house looks, it's sensible to check with Milton Keynes City Council's planning team first. We're happy to provide product details to help.

Newer homes on the edges of town

Olney has grown over the years, and the newer estates around the town have mostly attached or integral garages with standard-sized openings. Here the most popular upgrades are insulated sectional and roller doors, and fitting a motor so the door opens from the car. Many owners also take the chance to swap a plain white steel door for something in anthracite grey or a woodgrain effect.

Up-and-over doors and everyday repairs

Plenty of garages around Olney still have a steel up-and-over garage door, and they remain a sensible, good-value option when it's time to replace one. They're also the doors we repair most often. Common problems include:

  • A door that suddenly feels very heavy or won't stay open, often a broken spring
  • A frayed or snapped cable causing the door to hang crooked
  • Worn rollers, noisy runners and stiff or broken locks

Springs and cables are under tension and can cause serious injury, so please leave them to us. We repair all major makes and will always tell you honestly whether a repair or a new door makes more sense.

Measuring older openings properly

Old stone garages are rarely square, so we always measure in several places and check the reveals before ordering.

That's why we always measure in person before ordering. If an opening is out of square, we can use a made-to-measure door, adjust the frame, or add trims so the new door sits neatly and seals properly. Our page on bespoke and made-to-measure doors explains the options, and the garage door sizes guide shows how standard sizes compare.

Servicing to keep doors working

A periodic garage door service keeps older doors safe and smooth, and is particularly worthwhile on electric doors, where we test the safety reversing and any photocells as part of the visit. Our guide to maintaining your garage door explains the simple jobs you can safely do yourself in between.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Do you fit garage doors in the villages around Olney?

Yes. We cover the whole MK46 area, including Emberton, Weston Underwood, Lavendon, Clifton Reynes, Warrington and Ravenstone, as well as Olney itself. Many village properties have older detached garages or outbuildings, and we're used to measuring and fitting doors to non-standard openings. Just let us know your address when you get in touch and we'll arrange a convenient time to visit.

Do I need permission to change my garage door in Olney?

For most homes, replacing a garage door like-for-like doesn't need planning permission. If your property is listed, or you're making a significant change to its appearance in a conservation area, it's wise to check with Milton Keynes City Council before going ahead. We can't give planning advice, but we can supply product details and drawings to help with any application.

Is timber or GRP better for a period garage?

Both can look excellent. Timber has a natural, authentic feel and can be made to almost any size or design, but it needs re-finishing every few years. GRP gives a convincing woodgrain look with very little maintenance and won't rot or warp. If you're unsure, we can bring samples to your survey so you can see both against your stonework.

Can you automate a side-hinged garage door?

Often, yes. Side-hinged doors can be fitted with swing-arm or concealed operators, similar to those used on electric gates, provided the doors are sound and there's room for the arms to work. It's a little more involved than motorising an up-and-over or sectional door, so we'll assess your garage during the survey and explain the options and costs clearly.
