How Much Are Electric Garage Doors? UK Cost Guide

Indicative 2026 prices for electric roller, sectional, up and over and double doors, plus the cost of automating the door you already have.

So, how much are electric garage doors? As an indicative UK guide for 2026, a single electric garage door supplied and fitted typically costs around £1,300–£2,800 for an insulated roller door, £1,300–£2,300 for an electric up and over door, and £2,000–£3,800 for an insulated sectional door. Double electric garage doors usually cost roughly 40–70% more than a single. If you'd rather keep your existing door, automating it with a new motor typically costs around £550–£1,100 fitted. The final price depends on the size, door type, insulation, finish, brand and installation, and should always be confirmed after a free survey.

Below we break down electric garage door costs by type and size, the price of automating an existing door, the extras that add to the bill, and what affects the price.

Electric roller garage doors prices

Electric roller garage doors are one of the most popular choices because they roll up into a compact box and need no space inside the garage. Budget insulated aluminium doors sit at the lower end of the range; doors with a higher security rating, wider slats, premium colours or wood-effect finishes cost more. Electric roller garage doors prices in the UK also vary with the motor: a reliable tubular motor with soft-stop and obstruction detection is worth paying for.

Electric sectional garage doors prices

Sectional garage doors lift vertically then run back under the ceiling. They're well insulated, quiet and well sealed, so they cost more than most roller doors. Premium brands such as Hörmann sit towards the top of the range, especially with larger sizes, glazing or special finishes.

Electric up and over garage doors prices

An electric up and over garage door is often the most affordable way to get an automatic door, particularly in steel. Retractable doors suit motors best. Up and over doors aren't usually insulated, so if you use the garage as a workshop or gym, it's worth weighing this up.

Electric double garage doors fitted prices

Double doors need more material, a stronger motor and sometimes extra fixings or a stronger lintel. As an indicative UK guide for a standard double (around 14ft x 7ft):

Electric up and over door£2,000–£3,500Steel; heavier doors need a more powerful operator
Electric sectional door£3,000–£5,500Insulated; popular choice for wide openings

What affects electric garage door costs?

  • Size: wider and taller doors cost more, and made-to-measure doors carry a small premium. Our garage door sizes UK guide explains standard sizes.
  • Door type and material: steel up and over doors are usually cheapest; insulated sectional and timber side hinged doors are at the top end.
  • Insulation: insulated garage doors cost more but make the garage warmer, quieter and drier.
  • Security: doors tested to recognised security standards cost more than basic models.
  • Motor quality: soft start and stop, quieter running, obstruction detection and rolling-code remotes are worth having.
  • Brand: premium manufacturers cost more, often with longer warranties.
  • Installation: access, the condition of the opening, removal of the old door and any electrical work all affect the price.

Running and maintenance costs

Electric garage doors use very little electricity. A typical motor draws a small amount on standby and only uses noticeable power for the few seconds it's moving, so running costs are usually just a few pounds a year. The bigger cost over time is maintenance: a yearly garage door service typically costs around £80–£150 and helps catch worn parts before they fail. If something does go wrong, our guide on what to do when your electric garage door is not working covers common faults and fixes.

Electric garage door repair costs

It's also worth knowing what repairs typically cost, so you can budget over the life of the door. As an indicative UK guide, a replacement remote is usually around £40–£90 programmed, a pair of safety photocells £100–£200 fitted, and a replacement motor £350–£750 depending on the door type. Springs and cables on sectional and up and over doors typically cost £120–£300 to replace. These are guide figures only, confirmed after inspection. Regular servicing tends to reduce how often these bills come up.

Are electric garage doors worth the cost?

For many people, yes. You can open the door without getting out of the car in the rain, modern motors resist the door being forced up, and they're a real help for anyone with mobility issues. An insulated electric door also makes the garage more usable as a gym, workshop or storage space. If budget is tight, automating a sound existing door is a cost-effective way to get the same convenience.

Watch out for: very low headline prices that leave out removal of the old door, making good or a decent motor. Always compare like with like on a written quote, including the warranty on the door and motor.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How much does an electric garage door cost in the UK?

As an indicative 2026 UK guide, a single electric garage door supplied and fitted usually costs around £1,300–£2,800 for an insulated roller door, £1,300–£2,300 for an electric up and over door, and £2,000–£3,800 for an insulated sectional door. Doubles cost roughly 40–70% more. Your exact price is confirmed after a free survey.

How much does it cost to automate an existing garage door?

Fitting a motor to a suitable existing up and over or sectional door typically costs around £550–£1,000 fitted, and converting a manual roller door is often £500–£950. Side hinged automation is usually £1,000–£2,000. The door must be in good condition and well balanced, so we check it first and recommend any repairs needed.

Are electric roller garage doors cheaper than sectional doors?

Usually, yes. A single insulated electric roller door typically costs less than a comparable insulated sectional door, which uses heavier panels, tracks and springs. Premium roller doors with high security ratings or special finishes can overlap with sectional prices, though. The right choice depends on your headroom, budget and how you use the garage.

How much are electric double garage doors fitted?

As an indicative UK guide, an electric double garage door around 14ft x 7ft typically costs £2,200–£4,200 for a roller door, £2,000–£3,500 for a steel up and over door, and £3,000–£5,500 for an insulated sectional door, fitted. Wider doors, premium finishes and stronger motors push prices higher. A free survey confirms the exact figure.

Do electric garage doors cost a lot to run?

No. Electric garage door motors only draw significant power for the few seconds they're moving and use very little on standby, so running costs are usually just a few pounds a year. An annual service, typically around £80–£150, is a sensible ongoing cost that helps keep the motor and door running smoothly and safely.
