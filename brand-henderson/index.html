About Henderson

Henderson is a long-standing British brand, known for door gear and for garage doors, particularly steel up and over doors. Merlin is the Henderson range of garage door openers and remote controls, so if you have a Merlin handset, it is likely connected to a Henderson operator.

Because Henderson doors have been fitted for many years, we see a real mix, from older manual doors that just need new springs to automated doors that need an opener repair or a new remote.

Henderson doors we work on

Henderson up and over garage doors

Henderson up and over doors are the most common type we see. They use springs, and on some mechanisms cables, to balance the weight of the door so it lifts easily. Over time springs weaken or snap, cables fray and pivots wear. The result is a door that feels heavy, drops when it should stay open or lifts unevenly. Our up and over garage doors page explains canopy and retractable gear in more detail.

Henderson electric doors and Merlin openers

Many Henderson doors have been automated with Merlin openers. Typical issues include remotes that have stopped working, an opener that hums but does not move, a door that reverses before it closes and lost handsets that need replacing.

Repair or replace your Henderson door?

If the panel and frame are in good order, repairing a Henderson door is usually the sensible choice. A replacement is worth considering if the steel is rusted through, the door is badly dented, parts are unobtainable, or you want better insulation and security. Read our guide to repairing or replacing a garage door, and if you do choose a new door, compare steel up and overs from Garador or other classic British doors such as Cardale.

Signs your Henderson door needs attention

Older up and over doors tend to give plenty of warning before they fail. Look out for:

  • The door feeling noticeably heavier than it used to, or harder to close.
  • A twang or bang when opening, which often means a spring has snapped.
  • The door sitting lower on one side or rubbing on the frame.
  • Frayed cables, rust on the springs or a grinding noise from the pivots.
  • A Merlin opener that strains, stops part way or reverses unexpectedly.

If you notice any of these, stop using the door and get it checked before a small repair becomes a bigger one. A regular garage door service helps catch these problems early, and our guide to garage door maintenance covers simple checks you can safely do yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Is Merlin a Henderson product?

Yes. Merlin is the Henderson range of garage door openers and remote controls. If you have a Merlin handset or opener, it is part of the Henderson family of products. We can diagnose faults, reprogramme remotes and supply compatible replacement handsets, although very old openers may need checking for compatibility first.

My Henderson up and over door drops when I open it. Why?

An up and over door that will not stay open, or drops suddenly, usually has weak or broken springs, or a problem with the cables or pivots. It can fall without warning, so stop using it and keep people and cars clear. Springs are under high tension and must be replaced by a professional.

Can you get spare parts for Henderson garage doors?

For many Henderson doors, yes. Springs, cables, locks and handles are often available as original or compatible parts. Some older models are harder to supply, so we will identify your door and check before we quote. If the right parts are no longer available, we will price a replacement door so you can compare.

My Merlin remote has stopped working. What should I check?

First, try a fresh battery and check that the opener has power. If other remotes work, the handset may simply need reprogramming. If none work, the fault may be with the opener or receiver. Our guide to an electric garage door not working has more checks, or we can come out and diagnose it.

Do you service Henderson garage doors?

Yes. A service covers checking springs, cables, pivots and fixings, lubricating moving parts, adjusting the balance and testing locks and any opener safety features. Regular servicing helps spot worn parts before they fail and keeps the door lighter and quieter to use. We usually suggest an annual check for older doors.
