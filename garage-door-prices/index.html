Garage door prices in Milton Keynes: the quick answer

Most people want a ballpark figure before booking a survey, so we have put our typical garage door prices in Milton Keynes in one place. As a quick answer: a manual steel up and over door fitted usually costs from about £700, an electric insulated roller door from around £1,500, and an electric insulated sectional door from roughly £1,800 for a single garage. Doubles, timber and bespoke designs cost more.

Guide prices only: every figure on this page is an indicative 2026 range for a typical-sized opening, including supply, fitting, VAT and removal of the old door unless stated. Your actual price is confirmed in writing after a free, no-obligation survey.

What affects the price of a garage door?

  • Door type. Up and over doors are simplest to make and fit; sectional and roller doors have more components.
  • Material. Steel is the least expensive, then GRP, aluminium and timber, with hardwoods at the top.
  • Size. Double and extra-tall doors cost more, and made-to-measure sizes add to the price.
  • Insulation. Foam-filled panels add cost but improve comfort and reduce condensation.
  • Finish and colour. Standard white is cheapest; woodgrain effects and RAL colours cost more.
  • Operation. Electric operation, extra remotes, keypads and battery backup all add to the total.
  • Security. Upgraded locking or doors tested to recognised security standards cost more.
  • The opening. Damaged brickwork, out-of-square openings or a missing power supply can add work.

Is insulation worth the extra cost?

An insulated door usually adds a few hundred pounds compared with a single-skin steel door. Whether that is money well spent depends on how you use the garage. If it is attached to the house, has a room above it, or doubles as a gym, workshop or utility area, insulation makes it noticeably warmer, quieter and less prone to condensation. If the garage is detached and only used for storage, a single-skin door is often enough. Our guide on whether insulated garage doors are worth it goes into the detail.

Why quotes vary between companies

If you collect several quotes, you may see quite different figures for what looks like the same door. The difference usually comes down to the specification: the manufacturer and range, the panel thickness, the motor fitted, whether photocells and extra remotes are included, and whether removal and disposal are part of the price. When comparing, check each quote lists the exact door model, colour, size, operator and guarantee terms, so you are comparing like with like.

Prices across the wider area

Our guide prices apply across Milton Keynes and the towns we cover, including Bedford, Northampton, Aylesbury and Buckingham. The door itself costs the same wherever it is fitted; what changes the price is the opening, the specification and any preparation work, all of which we confirm at the survey.

How to save on a new garage door

  • Choose a standard size and a standard colour where you can.
  • Consider automating your existing door if it is in good shape, rather than replacing it.
  • Get a double door instead of two singles where the opening allows.
  • Have power installed in the garage before fitting day to avoid a second visit.
  • Service the door yearly; a small adjustment now often prevents a bigger repair later.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Are your garage door prices fixed?

The figures on this page are guide prices based on typical openings. We confirm your exact price in writing after a free, no-obligation survey, once we have measured the opening and discussed the door, finish and options you want. The quoted price is the price you pay unless you change the specification.

Do prices include fitting and removing the old door?

Yes, our guide prices for new doors include supply, fitting, VAT and removal and disposal of a standard existing door. Extra work such as repairing brickwork, fitting a new lintel or installing a power supply for an electric door is not included and would be shown separately on your quote.

What is the cheapest type of garage door?

A manual steel up and over door in a standard size and white finish is usually the cheapest option to buy and fit. It is a sound choice if you mainly need a secure, low-maintenance door. If you want warmth, quiet running or electric operation, an insulated roller door offers good value.

How much does it cost to make my garage door electric?

Adding a motor to a sound up and over or sectional door is typically between £500 and £950 as a guide, depending on the door style and extras. Converting a manual roller door or fitting operators to side hinged doors costs more. We confirm the exact price after checking your door.

Do you offer cheap garage doors in Milton Keynes?

We offer good-value options rather than the cheapest possible doors. A standard steel up and over door fitted properly is affordable and will last for many years. We also tell you honestly when a repair or automation is better value than a new door, so you only spend what you need to.
