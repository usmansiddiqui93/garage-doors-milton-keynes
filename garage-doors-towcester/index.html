Garage doors Towcester: town and country coverage

We provide garage doors Towcester homeowners can rely on, whether you live in the town centre or out in the surrounding countryside. Our fitters cover the NN12 postcode district, including Towcester itself and villages such as Silverstone, Paulerspury, Greens Norton, Blakesley, Tiffield, Shutlanger and Abthorpe. Towcester sits on the A5, the old Watling Street, which makes it a straightforward drive from Milton Keynes for surveys, installations and repairs.

Housing around Towcester

Towcester and its villages have a real variety of homes, and that affects which garage doors make sense:

  • Stone cottages and period houses in the town centre and villages, often with detached garages, stables or outbuildings.
  • Barn conversions and larger rural homes, where garages may have wide or tall openings.
  • Post-war and late 20th-century estates with attached garages and steel up-and-over doors.
  • New-build developments around the edges of town, mostly with integral or attached garages to standard sizes.

Doors for stone and period homes

Much of the older building in this part of Northamptonshire uses local stone, and a garage door needs to sit comfortably alongside it. Two options stand out.

Timber doors

Painted or stained timber garage doors are the traditional choice for cottages, barns and period houses. They can be made to measure for awkward openings and finished in heritage colours, and they're available as side-hinged pairs, up-and-overs or sectional doors.

GRP doors

If you want the look of timber without the regular painting, GRP garage doors are a smart alternative. Glass reinforced plastic is moulded with a realistic woodgrain, won't rot or rust, and suits exposed rural spots where weather takes its toll.

Double garage doors for larger homes

Many village and new-build homes around Towcester have double garages. We fit double garage doors either as one wide door or as two single doors side by side. A single wide door gives you more room to manoeuvre two cars; two singles mean you only open the side you need and keep a central pier for support. We'll talk through which suits your drive and vehicles.

Sectional and electric doors for new builds

On newer homes, insulated sectional garage doors are the most popular upgrade. They rise vertically and run back under the ceiling, so you can park close to the door, and their insulated panels help keep integral garages warmer. Most are fitted with a motor and remote control, and we also supply electric garage doors in roller and up-and-over styles.

Security for rural garages

Out in the villages, a garage can be some distance from the house and often holds bikes, tools and garden machinery. Upgrading an old door to one with multi-point locking and anti-lift protection makes a real difference, and so does replacing a tired side door. An electric door adds a further layer of protection, because the motor holds the door firmly shut when it's closed. If security is a priority, mention it at the survey and we'll show you the stronger options available.

Servicing and repairs

Garage doors are often the largest moving object in a home, and they work hard. A regular garage door service keeps springs, cables, rollers and motors in good order and checks that safety features on electric doors work properly. If something has already failed, we repair all major makes, from snapped springs and cables to broken locks, failed remotes and doors off their tracks. Springs and cables are under high tension, so please leave them to a professional.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Which villages around Towcester do you cover?

We cover the NN12 area, including Silverstone, Paulerspury, Greens Norton, Blakesley, Tiffield, Shutlanger and Abthorpe, as well as Towcester town itself. If you're in a smaller hamlet nearby, just get in touch with your postcode. The A5 gives us a quick route up from Milton Keynes, so arranging a survey or repair visit is rarely difficult.

Can you fit garage doors to a barn conversion?

Yes. Barn conversions often have wide, tall or arched openings, and we can supply made-to-measure timber or GRP doors, wide sectional doors or side-hinged pairs to suit. We'll take careful measurements and look at how the opening is built so the door is fixed securely. If your barn is listed, check with the local planning authority before changing the doors.

Should I choose one double door or two single doors?

A single wide door gives the most room to drive in and out, which helps with larger cars, and looks clean and modern. Two single doors keep a central pier, can be cheaper to automate per door, and mean you only open the side you need. Both work well; we'll look at your drive, garage width and vehicles before advising.

How often should a garage door be serviced?

For most homes, a professional service roughly once a year is sensible, particularly for electric doors or doors used several times a day. A service checks and adjusts springs or torsion, inspects cables and rollers, lubricates moving parts and tests safety features. Between services, keep tracks clean and listen out for new noises, which often signal wear before a failure.
