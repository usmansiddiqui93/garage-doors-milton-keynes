Wooden garage doors in Milton Keynes bring a warmth and character that painted metal simply can't copy. Whether it's a stone cottage in one of the north Bucks villages, an older property in Wolverton or a new-build that needs a little more personality, a well-made timber door lifts the whole frontage. We supply and fit wooden and timber garage doors in side hinged, up and over and sectional styles, finished to your choice of stain or paint.

Why choose a timber garage door?

Real wood ages gracefully, can be refinished again and again, and is the natural choice for traditional and period homes. It's also a renewable material when sourced from responsibly managed forests; ask us about certification (such as FSC or PEFC) on the doors we quote.

  • Authentic appearance that suits conservation areas and older properties.
  • Made to measure easily, including for the odd sizes found on older garages and outbuildings.
  • Repairable: scratches and small dents can be sanded and refinished rather than living with them.
  • Naturally insulating: solid timber is a better insulator than a single sheet of steel.
  • Endless finishes: natural oils, translucent stains or opaque paint in any colour.

The honest drawbacks

Timber is not maintenance free. Expect to recoat the finish every few years, depending on exposure and the product used; south and west-facing doors weather fastest. Wood moves slightly with the seasons, so a good door is designed with that in mind. Timber doors also cost more than equivalent steel doors, and they're heavier, which affects the choice of gear and motor. If you love the look but not the upkeep, a GRP garage door with a moulded woodgrain finish is worth comparing.

Styles of wooden garage door

Side hinged timber doors

The classic. A pair of timber leaves, often with vertical boarding, framed and braced or with raised panels. Easy walk-in access and no overhead gear. Our side hinged garage doors page goes into layouts and locking.

Timber up and over doors

A single timber panel on retractable gear. Designs range from vertical boarding to Georgian-style panels. Because of the weight, retractable gear with properly rated springs is essential.

Timber and timber-faced sectional doors

Wooden sectional garage doors give you timber's looks with the vertical-opening, well-sealed operation of a sectional. Some are solid timber; others are insulated steel panels with a real timber face, which keeps weight and movement down.

Glazed and pedestrian-door designs

Wood garage doors with windows suit carriage-house and barn-style designs. Some timber doors can also be made with a pedestrian door set into one leaf or panel.

Finishes, insulation, automation and security

Factory finished or site finished?

We recommend factory-finished doors wherever possible. Coatings applied in controlled conditions, on every face and edge before assembly, last much longer than paint brushed on after fitting. If your door arrives primed only, it must be fully finished promptly. Our guide can you paint garage doors? explains how to look after the finish.

Insulation

Timber naturally holds heat better than thin steel, and some doors add an insulated core or backing. Insulated wooden garage doors make sense for integral garages or workshops.

Automation

Electric wooden garage doors are entirely possible: we fit operators rated for the extra weight, or arm operators for side hinged leaves.

Security

A solid hardwood door with a multi-point lock and hinge bolts is a robust barrier. We fit good-quality locks and can add internal bolts on the fixed leaf.

Is a wooden garage door right for you?

  • You live in an older or listed home, or in a conservation area such as the historic centres of Olney, Stony Stratford or Newport Pagnell, where planning or appearance matters.
  • You want a door that matches timber windows, cladding or a front door.
  • You don't mind recoating every few years in return for a natural look.
  • You have a non-standard opening that needs a truly bespoke door; see bespoke garage doors.

If your property is listed, check with the local planning authority before changing the door's appearance.

Fitting a timber door

Timber doors are heavy and need a solid fixing. At survey we check that the reveals and lintel are sound and measure carefully, because made-to-measure timber doors can take several weeks to make. On the day we fit the frame, check it's square and plumb, hang the door, fit locks and seals and make sure every edge of the door is sealed and finished. We'll leave you with care advice for your chosen finish.

Repairs and maintenance for wooden doors

Our garage door repair service covers timber doors of all ages: dropped leaves, rotten bottom rails, failed hinges, sticking locks and swollen panels. Where the timber is sound, a repair and refinish can buy many more years. When rot is widespread, replacement is usually better value. We also offer servicing for the gear and springs on timber up and over doors.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How often does a wooden garage door need treating?

It depends on the finish and how exposed the door is. As a rough guide, translucent stains and oils may need refreshing every two to four years, while good-quality opaque paint systems often last longer. South and west-facing doors weather fastest. Recoating before the finish breaks down is quicker and cheaper than stripping back later.

Are timber garage doors more expensive than steel?

Yes, generally. A timber door typically costs more than a comparable steel door because of the material, the joinery involved and, often, the made-to-measure manufacture. Heavier timber doors may also need stronger gear or operators. Many people feel the appearance and character justify the extra cost, especially on period homes.

Can a wooden garage door be electric?

Yes. Timber up and over and sectional doors can be fitted with a ceiling-mounted operator rated for their weight, and side hinged timber doors can use arm operators. The door must be well balanced and the operator sized correctly. We'll recommend the right motor when we survey your garage.

Yes. We supply and fit wooden and timber garage doors in Bedford, Northampton and Aylesbury as well as across Milton Keynes. Whether you need a traditional side hinged pair or a timber-faced sectional door, the process starts with a free survey and a clear written quote.
