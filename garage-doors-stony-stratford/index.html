Garage doors Stony Stratford homes deserve

We supply, fit and repair garage doors Stony Stratford homeowners are proud of, whether that's a hand-finished timber door for a Georgian townhouse or a quiet electric door for a family home on one of the estates. Our team covers the whole of the MK11 postcode district, including the historic town centre, Fullers Slade and Galley Hill, and we regularly work in nearby villages across the border in Northamptonshire such as Old Stratford, Deanshanger and Cosgrove, as well as Calverton to the south.

A historic High Street on Watling Street

Stony Stratford grew up on the old Roman road of Watling Street, and its High Street is lined with Georgian and earlier buildings, including the coaching inns linked to the phrase "a cock and bull story". Behind the frontages you'll find yards, outbuildings and garages that were never designed with modern doors in mind.

Wooden garage doors

For period properties, wooden garage doors are usually the first choice. Painted softwood or stained hardwood looks right against old brick and stone, and timber can be made to fit almost any opening. With sensible upkeep, a good timber door will last for many years.

Bespoke and made-to-measure doors

Older openings are often oddly sized, arched or out of square. Our bespoke garage doors are made to your exact measurements, so there are no unsightly packers or gaps, and you can choose the design, finish and hardware to suit the house.

Conservation area: much of central Stony Stratford is a conservation area and some buildings are listed. If your property is listed, or you're planning a noticeable change to the front of the house, check with Milton Keynes City Council's planning team first.

Fullers Slade, Galley Hill and newer homes

The Milton Keynes estates around the town, built from the 1970s onwards, have a mix of attached garages and shared garage blocks. Many still have tired steel up-and-over doors that are due for replacement. On the newer homes, owners tend to want warmth, security and convenience, which usually means an insulated sectional or roller door, often motorised.

Electric doors and automation

Pulling up on a wet evening and opening the garage from the car is a small luxury that soon feels essential. We supply and fit electric garage doors in roller, sectional and up-and-over styles, with remote controls, safety reversing and a manual release in case of a power cut.

If your current door is still in good shape, it may be possible to keep it and add a motor instead. Our garage door automation service fits operators to suitable existing doors, after checking the door is balanced and safe to automate. It's often a cost-effective middle ground between doing nothing and buying a new door.

Double garages in the surrounding villages

Larger homes in Old Stratford, Deanshanger and the surrounding countryside often have double garages. We fit double garage doors as a single wide door or as two singles, and we can advise on which works better for your driveway, the cars you own and how you use the space. Wide sectional doors are particularly popular because they're strong, well insulated and don't swing out.

Repairs to all makes

Older doors in the town centre and on the estates alike take a lot of wear. From snapped springs and cables to failed motors, jammed locks and damaged panels, we repair all major makes of garage door, and we carry the most common parts on the van so many faults can be put right in a single visit. If a door isn't worth repairing, we'll say so and price a replacement alongside.

Nearby areas we cover

Stony Stratford is on the north-western edge of Milton Keynes, a short drive for our fitters. We also cover Wolverton next door and Towcester a few miles up the A5. See our full list of areas we cover.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Do you cover the villages near Stony Stratford in Northamptonshire?

Yes. Although they fall across the county border, villages such as Old Stratford, Deanshanger and Cosgrove are only a few minutes from Stony Stratford and we work there regularly. We also cover Calverton and the surrounding countryside. Just tell us your postcode when you get in touch and we'll arrange a survey at a time that suits you.

Can you make a door for an arched or unusual opening?

Yes. Made-to-measure timber doors can be built to suit arched heads, unusual widths and openings that are slightly out of square. In some cases a roller or sectional door can be fitted behind the opening instead. We take careful measurements during the survey and talk you through the options so the finished door looks as though it was always meant to be there.

How much does it cost to automate an existing garage door?

As a rough guide, fitting a motor to a suitable existing up-and-over or sectional door typically costs around £500 to £900, depending on the door, the operator chosen and whether power needs running to the garage. This is an indicative range only; we confirm the price after a free survey, including checking the door is in good enough condition to automate.

Will an electric garage door still open in a power cut?

Yes. Electric doors we fit have a manual release or override so you can open and close them by hand if the power goes off. On roller doors this is often a hand crank or a quick-release mechanism, and on sectional and up-and-over doors it's usually a release cord. We'll show you how it works when we hand the door over.
