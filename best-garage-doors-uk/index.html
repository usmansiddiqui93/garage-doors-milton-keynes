The Best Garage Doors in the UK: How to Choose

There is no single best garage door, but there is a best door for your garage. Here is how to pick the right type, material and features for how you actually use it.

The best garage doors in the UK are the ones that suit your garage and how you use it. For most homes, an insulated electric sectional or roller door is the best all-rounder: it keeps the drive clear, seals well and is easy to use every day. If budget is the priority, a good steel up and over door is the best value. If you want character, a well-made timber or timber-effect side hinged door is hard to beat. Quality of installation matters as much as the door itself.

Below we break down the best choice for each common need, what to look for in a well-made door, and the questions worth asking before you buy.

Best sectional garage doors

The best sectional garage doors have thick, foam-filled steel panels, smooth-running rollers on robust tracks, good finger-trap protection between panels and effective seals at the top, sides and bottom. Look for well-designed spring systems with safety features if a spring or cable breaks. A choice of panel designs, colours and windows lets you match the door to the house. If you are comparing models, our sectional garage doors page explains the options in more detail.

Best roller garage doors

The best roller garage doors in the UK use double-skinned aluminium slats filled with insulating foam, strong guide rails, a good brush or rubber seal in the guides and a flexible bottom seal to cope with uneven floors. A quality tubular motor with obstacle detection and a reliable manual override is essential. For the best roller shutter garage doors on security, look for models certified to standards such as LPS 1175. Compare options on our roller garage doors page.

Best electric garage doors

What makes the best electric garage door is mainly the operator: a quiet, well-made motor with soft start and stop, reliable safety reversal, rolling-code or encrypted remotes, and a clear way to open the door manually in a power cut. Sectional and roller doors are designed around electric operation, but up and over and side hinged doors can be automated well too. Our guide to how much electric garage doors cost covers the price difference.

Best insulated garage doors

If your garage is attached to the house, has a room above it or is used as a workshop, insulation is worth paying for. The best insulated garage doors for the UK are sectional doors with thick foam-filled panels and continuous seals, followed closely by insulated roller doors. Single-skin steel doors offer little insulation. Our guide are insulated garage doors worth it? helps you decide.

Best garage doors for security

Most modern garage doors are reasonably secure when properly fitted, but some go further. For the best garage doors for security, look for doors independently tested to standards such as LPS 1175 (which rates resistance to attack with specific tools for a set time) or PAS 24, and check that the door has anti-lift protection and strong locking. On up and over doors, adding extra locks can make a real difference. See our page on secure garage doors for more.

Best side hinged garage doors

The best side hinged doors have a rigid frame, strong hinges, good weather seals and a quality multipoint lock, with bolts on the fixed leaf. Timber suits traditional homes, while steel and GRP suit modern ones and need far less maintenance. An unequal pair (one wide leaf, one narrow) is handy if you often walk in and out.

Best wood for garage doors

For timber garage doors, the best woods are durable species that resist rot and movement. Hardwoods such as oak and iroko are strong and long-lasting but cost more. Western red cedar is light, stable and naturally rot-resistant. Quality treated softwoods are the budget choice but need more upkeep. Engineered timber (laminated in layers) resists warping better than solid boards. Whatever the wood, it needs a good finish, refreshed every few years.

Best value garage doors

Best value is not the same as cheapest. A basic steel up and over door from a reputable manufacturer, fitted properly, will usually outlast a cheaper door that is poorly installed. Value comes from:

  • A door from an established manufacturer with spare parts available.
  • Correct measuring and fitting into a sound frame and opening.
  • A clear written quote that includes removal of the old door.
  • Guarantee details you understand.

Our garage door prices guide gives typical costs for each type.

Which garage door brands are best?

Several established manufacturers supply the UK market, including large European groups and long-standing British brands. Each has strengths: some are known for broad sectional ranges, others for roller doors or classic steel up and overs. Rather than chasing a single best brand, choose the right type of door, then compare well-supported brands within it. You can read about the main makes on our garage door brands page.

What is the new trend in garage doors?

Current garage door trends in the UK include:

  • Anthracite grey and black: matching modern windows and front doors.
  • Smooth, flush or wide-ribbed panels: clean, minimal looks on sectional doors.
  • Woodgrain effects: warm timber looks with steel's low maintenance.
  • Glazed panels: windows or vision strips to bring light into the garage.
  • Smart control: app-connected operators and integration with home systems.

Browse styles and colours on our modern garage doors page. A tidy, well-chosen garage door can improve kerb appeal, which is worth bearing in mind if you plan to sell.

Best paint and lubricant for garage doors

For metal doors, use a paint designed for exterior metal, applied over a suitable primer on a clean, lightly keyed surface. For timber, use a microporous exterior paint or stain that lets the wood breathe. Our guide can you paint garage doors? explains the process. For lubrication, a silicone-based or light lithium spray on hinges, rollers and pivots works well, but keep roller door guide channels clean rather than greased, and never lubricate or adjust springs under tension yourself.

Why installation matters as much as the door

Even the best garage door will disappoint if it is fitted badly. A door that is not square in the opening will bind, wear its rollers and leak at the seals. Springs set to the wrong tension make a door heavy or let it drift, and an operator working against a poorly balanced door will fail early. A good installer will check the opening is sound, measure carefully, fix the frame securely into masonry, set the springs and operator correctly and show you how to use the manual release. That is why we always survey before quoting, rather than pricing from a photo.

Before you buy: ask what is included (removal, making good, electrics), how long the guarantee lasts, whether spare parts are readily available, and whether the price is fixed after survey.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

What is the best type of garage door?

For most UK homes, an insulated electric sectional door is the best all-round type. It lifts vertically so the drive stays clear, seals well on all sides and comes in many styles. A roller door is best where space is tight, and a steel up and over door is the best value if budget comes first.

Which garage doors are best for insulation in the UK?

Insulated sectional doors with thick foam-filled steel panels and four-sided seals are generally the best for insulation, closely followed by insulated aluminium roller doors. Fit matters too: gaps around the frame or a worn bottom seal will undo much of the benefit. Single-skin steel up and over doors offer very little insulation.

Are electric garage doors worth it?

For many people, yes. An electric door is easier and safer to use, especially in bad weather, and many operators add security by holding the door closed. They do cost more upfront and need occasional servicing. If you use your garage every day, or a family member finds a manual door heavy, an electric door is usually worth it.

What is the best value garage door?

A steel up and over door from an established manufacturer is usually the best value garage door. It costs less than sectional or roller doors, is simple to maintain and can be automated later. Value also depends on the fitting, so choose an installer who measures properly, gives a clear written quote and explains the guarantee.

What is the most secure garage door?

The most secure garage doors are those tested and certified to recognised security standards such as LPS 1175 or PAS 24, with anti-lift protection and robust locking. Some roller and sectional models carry these certifications. Correct installation into sound masonry is just as important. See our guide on how to secure a garage door for more.
