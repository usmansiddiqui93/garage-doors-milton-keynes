Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

How much are garage doors in the UK?

As a broad guide, a manual steel up and over door typically costs around £750–£1,400 fitted, while insulated electric roller and sectional doors are often £1,600–£3,800 fitted. Timber, bespoke and double doors cost more. These are indicative ranges; see our garage door prices page and get a free survey for an exact figure.

Who repairs garage doors?

Garage doors are best repaired by a specialist garage door engineer rather than a general handyman, because springs, cables and motors need specific knowledge and tools. Look for someone who repairs all major makes, explains the fault clearly and gives you a price before starting. Our garage door repairs page explains what we cover.

Can I repair my garage door myself?

Some simple jobs are fine for a confident DIYer, such as cleaning tracks, lubricating hinges and rollers, replacing remote batteries or tightening loose fixings. Anything involving springs, cables or the tension system should be left to a professional, because these parts store a lot of energy and can cause serious injury if released.
